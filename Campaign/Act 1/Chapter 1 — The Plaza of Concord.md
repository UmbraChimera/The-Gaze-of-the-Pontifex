# 🌇 Chapter 1 — The Plaza of Concord
*“Sixteen voices rose beneath the Spire, but only one spoke for the people.”*

---

## Introduction
This chapter begins the story of *The Gaze of the Pontifex* as the player characters arrive at **the Plaza of Concord**, the spiritual and political heart of [[Respite]].  
Thousands gather at dawn to hear **[[Serenya Vaelith]]**, priestess of [[Lathander]], call for reform, charity, and unity.  
Unbeknownst to the crowd — and the party — the rally will end in violence and transformation.  

This chapter introduces Respite’s tone: grand, holy, and deeply human. The PCs are placed in the heart of history as it unfolds.  

---

## The Plaza of Concord

**Location Summary:**  
A massive, circular plaza paved in white marble, radiating outward from the base of the [[Spire]].  
Sixteen great banners hang from the surrounding balustrades, each representing a god of the Conclave. The banners form a ring around a raised wooden dais draped in white and rose — the stage where Serenya will speak.

### Sensory Details
| Sense | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Sight** | The Spire towers above, glowing faintly in the morning sun. Pilgrims crowd every inch of space, some on balconies, others clinging to rooftops for a better view. |
| **Sound** | Temple bells, chanting, the crackle of sermons from street preachers, and the hiss of steam vents below. |
| **Smell** | A blend of incense, sweat, and forge oil from the distant [[Hammergate]] district. |
| **Feeling** | The air hums with energy — divine resonance and political anticipation intertwined. |

### Key Features
- **The Dais:** The focus of the rally. Easily visible to all.  
- **The Spire:** Looms behind Serenya like a divine backdrop.  
- **The Steps of the Conclave:** Marble terraces where clergy and nobles gather.  
- **The Faithful’s Circle:** Where pilgrims, workers, and the poor crowd shoulder-to-shoulder.  
- **Guard Lines:** Sanctified Guards form a perimeter at each entryway.

## Arriving at the Rally

### Read-Aloud Introduction
> The morning sun rises behind the Spire, its white stone blazing like molten silver.  
> All of Respite has gathered to witness a sermon unlike any other — a promise of renewal, perhaps rebellion.  
> Merchants call blessings, guards adjust their armor, and somewhere a street preacher shouts that the gods are watching.  
>   
> At the center of it all, a dais gleams beneath banners of rose and gold. Upon it stands a woman whose name the city chants: *“Serenya! Serenya! Serenya!”*  

Encourage each player to briefly describe their reason for being there.  
Are they assigned to security, drawn by faith, sent by a guild, or simply curious?

## Managing the Scene

### The Crowd
Treat the crowd as a living entity with moods that shift based on events and player choices.  

| Crowd State   | Trigger                                | Player Influence                                                               |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hopeful**   | Serenya begins speech.                 | Helping the weak, sharing food, or calming arguments reinforces this.          |
| **Restless**  | Mentions of curfews and punishment.    | PCs can soothe tempers (Persuasion DC 13) or stir unrest (Intimidation DC 13). |
| **Explosive** | Gunshot or perceived Guard aggression. | PCs can direct or protect fleeing civilians.                                   |

### Sanctified Guard Behavior
- Neutral but tense. They’re under strict orders not to provoke the crowd.  
- If a riot starts, they’ll form a shield wall and begin dispersal using nonlethal force.  
- The Guard’s loyalty lies with [[Helm]] and the Pontifex, not Serenya.

---

## Key Environmental Skill Opportunities
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice tension in the Guard line, restless murmurs.  
- **Insight DC 13:** Sense political fear — many clergy are here “to be seen,” not to listen.  
- **Religion DC 12:** Recognize sermon structure — Serenya’s invoking ancient Lathanderite rhetoric of renewal.  
- **History DC 15:** Recall prior “reform sermons” that ended in riots, decades ago.  

Allow players to use these checks to feel the scene — not to find answers yet.

---

## Serenya’s Speech
**Tone:** Warm, commanding, prophetic — but never revolutionary. She speaks as one calling the Pontifex *back to mercy*, not condemning him.

> “Brothers and sisters of the light, citizens of Respite, children of the Covenant!  
>  
> I stand beneath the Spire, as our ancestors once did when the gods healed the world.  
> But what peace is there now, when the worker fears the dusk bell, and mothers huddle beneath curfew’s shadow?  
>  
> The Pontifex tells us that stillness is safety — yet peace built on silence is not the peace of the gods.  
> The forges of Hammergate stand cold, the hungry wait for dawn that never comes.  
>  
> Faith without mercy is tyranny by another name.”  

At this point, the crowd cheers — or murmurs nervously. Then:  

> “The gods did not bind us to kneel — they raised us to build! The dawn belongs to—”  

The rifle shot cuts her off.

## Transition to Chapter 2: The Shot
**Trigger:** The sniper fires. Serenya collapses into Alaric’s arms.  
The sound of the gun echoes against the Spire and across the city.  

Immediately describe:  
- Guards forming up.  
- Civilians panicking.  
- Bells from every temple tower ringing in alarm.  

**Skill Checks to React:**
| Action | Skill | DC | Result |
|--------|--------|----|--------|
| Spot shooter | Perception | 17 | Brief flash of movement on a rooftop. |
| Aid Serenya | Medicine | 15 | Her blood glows faintly gold. |
| Rally crowd | Persuasion | 14 | Prevents trampling, buys time for Guard. |
| Protect dais | Athletics | 13 | Keep mob from swarming the stage. |

End the chapter on the realization that Serenya has been shot and the plaza is erupting into chaos.

---

## Chapter End Summary
- Serenya wounded, bleeding gold.  
- The crowd breaks.  
- The Sanctified Guard seals the exits.  
- PCs choose their first act: protect, pursue, or survive.  

Transition directly to **Chapter 2 — The Shot**.

> *“The first light of dawn burned red upon the Spire, and Respite’s prayers turned to screams.”*
