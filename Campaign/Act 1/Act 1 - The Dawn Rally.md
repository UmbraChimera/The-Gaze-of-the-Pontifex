# 🕊️ Act 1 — The Dawn Rally
*An opening act of faith, fire, and fracture.*

---

## Introduction
The city-state of **Respite** gleams beneath the gaze of the gods. Sixteen faiths govern as one, each voice united under the Pontifex, the mortal vessel of divine harmony. For centuries, peace has held, but beneath the silver banners and marble facades, the hum of dissent grows louder.  

The people are hungry. The rail forges of [[Hammergate]] cool at dusk. The laws grow stricter, the curfews longer, and the eyes of the [[Sanctified Guard]] never close.  

Into this tension rises [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith]], the half-elven priestess of [[Lathander]] known for her compassion and charisma. On the first morning of Brightmarch, she will stand before the [[Spire]] to demand mercy and reform - an event that will shake the Covenant to its core.

---

## Running This Act
This act serves as the **inciting event** for the campaign *The Gaze of the Pontifex*.  
The adventure begins at 5th level and should conclude around 6th or 7th, depending on the party’s engagement with the aftermath.

**Themes:**
- **Faith vs. Authority:** Questioning the sanctity of divine power.  
- **Hope vs. Control:** Can compassion survive under holy bureaucracy?  
- **Truth vs. Belief:** What happens when miracles are manufactured?  

**Tone:**
Blend political intrigue with civic tension and street-level heroism. The act should feel alive with chants, sermons, and smoke — the sound of civilization teetering between devotion and despair.

---

## Story Overview
- **Opening Scene:** The PCs attend or guard the rally known as *The Dawn March* (later called *The Dawn Rally* after the violence).  
- **Inciting Incident:** Serenya is shot mid-speech in front of thousands.  
- **Chaos:** The crowd riots, the Guard locks down the plaza, and confusion reigns.  
- **Aftermath:** Serenya miraculously survives, hailed as a living saint.  
- **Consequence:** New curfews and inquisitions follow, deepening the divide between Reformers and Traditionalists.  

By the end of Act 1, the party will have chosen where they stand in this struggle — or been chosen by it.

## Adventure Structure

| Chapter | Title | Summary |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **The Plaza of Concord** | The PCs arrive at the rally, experience the tension, and witness the speech. |
| 2 | **The Shot** | A sniper strikes; chaos erupts. The party reacts amid panic. |
| 3 | **The Seal of Silence** | The Guard locks down the plaza; PCs aid civilians, heal Serenya, or chase the shooter. |
| 4 | **The Hall of Concord** | PCs are questioned or praised; factions begin recruiting. |

---

## Marks of the City
Respite should feel like a theocratic metropolis at the height of tension.  
Use sensory cues and contradictions — sacred and industrial, divine and decaying — to convey tone.

- **Sights:** Steam pipes under stained glass. Sanctified Guards marching beside beggars.  
- **Sounds:** Chiming bells blending with hammer strikes. Preachers competing with factory whistles.  
- **Smells:** Incense, forge smoke, oil, and bread.  
- **Feel:** Holy order on the surface, fear beneath.  

---

## Chapter 1 — The Plaza of Concord
**Setting:** A vast open square at the Spire’s base, filled with thousands of citizens.  

**Read-Aloud (Paraphrasable):**
> The dawn bells ring nine times. The Spire gleams above you like a blade of light. Banners of sixteen gods ripple overhead, forming a circle of color against the white sky. The crowd murmurs as a golden-haired priestess steps onto a raised dais, her robes radiant with the sigil of the Morninglord. The chant rises: “Light for all!”  

**Key NPCs Present:**  
- [[Serenya Vaelith]] — delivers the speech.  
- [[Alaric Dathen]] — her loyal protector.  
- Five personal guards (two clerics, two Sanctified soldiers, Alaric).  
- [[High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil]] — overseeing plaza security from the edge.  

**Player Hooks:**
- *The Guard:* Assigned to security detail by Helm’s Watchers or Torm’s Loyal Furies.  
- *The Faithful:* Drawn by Serenya’s charity and reformist sermons.  
- *The Skeptic:* Investigating unrest or protecting a guild’s interest.  
- *The Outsider:* Simply caught in the crowd when history changes.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Speech
Serenya’s address combines spiritual fire and political daring. The players may sense tension in the crowd or spot figures on nearby rooftops.  

**Key Moments:**
- A speech check or passive Insight (DC 13) notes that Serenya rehearsed these words many times.  
- A Sanctified Guard whispers to another: “Too bold… the Pontifex will hear of this.”  
- If the PCs scan rooftops, Perception DC 15 spots a flash of metal — too late.

**The Gunshot:**
> A rifle’s crack splits the air. Serenya jerks, blood blooming against the sunburst on her robe. She gasps mid-word, falling into Alaric’s arms. Bells scream from every temple tower as the crowd erupts into chaos.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Seal of Silence
The [[Sanctified Guard]] floods the square, shields locking. The crowd breaks apart like waves against a wall of silver-blue armor.  

**Possible Actions:**
- **Rescue Civilians:** Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) DC 13 to drag people free.  
- **Aid Serenya:** Medicine DC 15 or magic; note the wound glows faintly gold.  
- **Pursue the Shooter:** Perception DC 17 to spot movement; chase leads to rooftops or sewers.  
- **Deescalate:** Persuasion DC 14 to calm the mob; failure incites further panic.  

**GM Note:**  
The shooter cannot be caught — he dies unseen. The real aim was spectacle, not murder.  

When order returns, the Guard seals the plaza, invoking the *Seal of Silence* — all witnesses are detained “for questioning.”

---

## Chapter 4 — The Hall of Concord
The PCs are brought before Conclave officials for questioning or commendation.  
Serenya lies in recovery, tended by Reformist clergy.  

**Events:**
- [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] orders an inquiry, while Reformers argue for leniency.  
- [[Alaric Dathen]] thanks the PCs personally and asks them to “stay close — she trusts you.”  
- PCs can gather information, earn favor with factions, or notice inconsistencies in reports.

**Faction Seeds:**
- **Reformers:** Invite the PCs to join the cause.  
- **Traditionalists:** Offer employment or surveillance tasks.  
- **The Guard:** Ask the party to serve as neutral investigators.  

---

## Chapter 5 — The City at Dusk
By sundown, Respite has changed.  
Curfews tighten, sermons harden, and rumors spread faster than truth. The PCs become symbols — witnesses to the event that birthed *The Saint of the Dawn*.

> “The bells no longer ring for prayer,” an old vendor mutters. “They ring to warn us.”

From here, the campaign diverges: Act 2 follows investigation, faith, or conspiracy depending on who the players side with.

| **Coronal Thalanil Aurelion** | Pontifex of Respite | Cold, divine, and detached; misreads the people’s unrest. |
| **High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil** | Commander of the Guard | Upholds order at any cost; a pragmatic zealot. |

---

## Adventure Hooks (for PCs)
1. **The Faithful:** You followed Serenya’s sermons; her survival is proof of Lathander’s favor.  
2. **The Guard:** Ordered to protect the rally; now responsible for the investigation.  
3. **The Laborer:** A Hammergate worker inspired by her promises of reform.  
4. **The Outsider:** In Respite for trade or faith — now trapped by curfews.  
5. **The Scholar:** Hired to transcribe the speech; notices the missing words after the shot.

---

## Closing of Act 1
Act 1 concludes when the PCs realize the assassination has changed Respite forever.  
The [[Conclave of Faiths]] fractures internally; whispers of reform and rebellion fill the air.  

Serenya becomes the saint the city never asked for, and the eyes of the Pontifex — both holy and human — now turn toward the party.

> **End of Act 1: “The Dawn Rally.”**
