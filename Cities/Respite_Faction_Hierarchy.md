# 🏛️ Respite Governance Framework
Every faction answers to layers of authority.  

### Layers
1. Faction: guild, union, embassy, etc.  
2. Initial Governing Body: tribunal, city watch, embassy guards.  
3. Escalation: temple of the appropriate Conclave deity.  

---

## 🛠️ Crafts & Industry
- Hammergate Unionists → union councils → [[Moradin]].  
- Glasswrights’ Guild → craft tribunal → [[Mystra]].  
- Masons’ Guild → masons’ court → [[Moradin]].
