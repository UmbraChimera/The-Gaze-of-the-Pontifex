# 🏰 Respite, the Holy City-State
**Motto:** *“All faiths rise beneath the [[Spire]].”*  
**Population:** ~500,000 in walls, ~1.5M in outer lands.  
**Government:** Theocratic conclave led by the [[Factions/Pontifex of Respite|Pontifex of Respite]]
**Current Pontifex:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]]

---

## 📍 Location
On the north shore of the Dragon Reach, between Procampur and Tsurlagol. The River Aurelion flows through the city.  

---

## 🛡️ Layout of Respite
- **Pilgrims’ Quarter** — hostels, shrines, and scams.  
- **Hammergate** — industry, rails, ritual lattice.  
- **Garden of the Gods** — seat of the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
- **Crown of Faith** — inner sanctum, palace, and [[Spire]].  

---

## 📖 Founding Myth & Truth
- Faithful lore: The Spire erupted when gods healed the Spellplague.  
- Hidden truth: The Spire is a divine **lock** for [[Tharizdun]]. The rail network doubles as ritual lines to break it.  

---

## 🕵️ Hooks
- [[Sanctified Guard]] patrols everywhere.  
- Nobles, guilds, and reformers clash.  
- Spirals appear in mosaics and relief art.
