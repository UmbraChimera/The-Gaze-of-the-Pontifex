## Serenya Vaelith — The Chained Prophet
**Deity:** [[Deities/Tharizdun|Tharizdun]] 
**Race:** Half-Elf 
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
**Title:** High Prophet of the Spiral
**Bloc:** None (hidden)

### Public Reputation
None. Her true identity as the Chained Prophet is a secret. If revealed, her betrayal would shatter Respite’s faith in divine unity.

### Personality
Calm and merciless. She sees destruction as salvation and the end of all suffering.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Soft-spoken, almost tender. Speaks of annihilation as one might speak of mercy.

### Goals
- Complete the Rite of the Spiral.  
- Sacrifice [[Alaric Dathen|Sir Alaric Dathen]] to break the divine lock.  
- Merge divine and mortal realms through Tharizdun’s avatar.  

### Relationships
- Former ally to [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]].  
- Manipulates [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Grainmother Alliera]] and the faithful through her charities.  

### Secrets
- Knows the true purpose of the [[Cities/Spire|Spire]].  
- Her death would not end the ritual—only delay it.
