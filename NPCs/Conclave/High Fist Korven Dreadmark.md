## High Fist Korven Dreadmark
**Deity:** [[Deities/Bane|Bane]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
**Title:** High Fist Korven Dreadmark of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Outsider  

### Public Reputation
Feared by nearly everyone but tolerated for his efficiency. His presence keeps the Guard disciplined.

### Personality
Cold, militaristic, and ambitious. Believes strength is the only truth.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Voice like a blade scraping stone. Rarely speaks without commanding someone.

### Goals
- Extend Bane’s influence through fear.
- Control the criminal underworld via intimidation.
- Keep Reformers in check.

### Relationships
- Manipulates the [[Sanctified Guard|Sanctified Guard]].
- Disliked by [[High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook|Ysella]].

### Secrets
- Secretly blackmails several Guard captains and funds the Lantern Syndicate.
