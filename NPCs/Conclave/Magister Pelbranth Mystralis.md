## Magister Pelbranth Mystralis
**Deity:** [[Deities/Mystra|Mystra]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Title:** Magister Pelbranth Mystralis of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Neutral  

### Public Reputation
Seen as the keeper of secrets and balance. His neutrality earns both respect and suspicion.

### Personality
Wise and patient but manipulative when it serves magical equilibrium.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Deep, resonant voice. Often distracted mid-conversation by unseen calculations.

### Goals
- Preserve the Weave.
- Prevent misuse of arcane power.
- Keep peace between divine and arcane factions.

### Relationships
- Mediator between [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]] and [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Serenya]].

### Secrets
- Keeps forbidden texts on the true origins of the Spire sealed in his archives.
