## Loremaster Daelen Quillborn
**Deity:** [[Deities/Oghma|Oghma]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Title:** Loremaster Daelen Quillborn of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Neutral  

### Public Reputation
Considered the mind of the Conclave. Respected but aloof.

### Personality
Detached and curious. Cares more for truth than politics.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Soft voice, constant note-taking. Occasionally mutters corrections mid-debate.

### Goals
- Record every conclave decree.
- Maintain archives without bias.
- Protect knowledge from destruction.

### Relationships
- Neutral mediator between factions.
- Friend of [[NPCs/Conclave/Mystra_Pelbranth|Pelbranth]].

### Secrets
- Keeps hidden records detailing inconsistencies in the city’s founding myth.
