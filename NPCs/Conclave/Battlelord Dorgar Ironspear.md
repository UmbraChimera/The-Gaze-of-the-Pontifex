## Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear
**Deity:** [[Deities/Tempus|Tempus]]  
**Race:** Dwarf  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Title:** Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Wildcard  

### Public Reputation
Popular among guards and soldiers for his valor. Critics call him reckless.

### Personality
Gruff and straightforward; believes conflict sharpens character.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Laughs loudly, slaps shoulders when speaking, quick to challenge others to friendly contests.

### Goals
- Keep Respite militarily ready.
- Ensure the Sanctified Guard never grows complacent.

### Relationships
- Close to [[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral]].
- Openly clashes with [[High Radiance Selarra Flamehair|High Radiance Selarra Flamehair]].

### Secrets
- Keeps a bloodstained axe as a relic of his first battle within the Spire’s shadow.
