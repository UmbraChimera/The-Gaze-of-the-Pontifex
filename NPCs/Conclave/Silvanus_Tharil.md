## Warden Tharil Mossheart
**Deity:** [[Deities/Silvanus|Silvanus]]  
**Race:** Firbolg  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Title:** Warden Tharil Mossheart of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Neutral  

### Public Reputation
Seen as an outsider among clergy but respected for his wisdom.

### Personality
Gentle and patient. Prefers silence and observation over speech.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks slowly, uses natural metaphors. Keeps a crow familiar on his shoulder.

### Goals
- Preserve balance between nature and city.
- Protect Respite’s gardens and river.
- Resist pollution from Hammergate.

### Relationships
- Supports [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Alliera]]’s reforms.
- Opposes [[NPCs/Conclave/Moradin_Baelrik|Baelrik]]’s expansionism.

### Secrets
- Feels the plants near the Spire 'listen' when no one speaks.
