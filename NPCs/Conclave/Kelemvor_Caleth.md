## Gravewarden Caleth Veynar
**Deity:** [[Deities/Kelemvor|Kelemvor]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Title:** Gravewarden Caleth Veynar of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Wildcard  

### Public Reputation
Feared yet respected. His calm demeanor comforts mourners but unsettles politicians.

### Personality
Quiet and contemplative, believes all debts—including life—must be paid.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks with calm authority, rarely raising his voice. Occasionally stares into the distance as if seeing spirits.

### Goals
- Maintain the sanctity of death.
- Prevent resurrection abuse.
- Investigate anomalies beneath the Spire.

### Relationships
- Advisor to [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].
- Quietly aids [[NPCs/Conclave/Mystra_Pelbranth|Pelbranth]]’s investigations.

### Secrets
- Knows that something 'alive' stirs deep under the Spire but refuses to act without proof.
