## Justiciar Jorven Harth
**Deity:** [[Deities/Tyr|Tyr]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Title:** Justiciar Jorven Harth of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Traditionalist  

### Public Reputation
Respected as the moral backbone of the Conclave. Known for his fairness and incorruptibility, though many find him severe.

### Personality
Stern and principled. Believes justice must be absolute, not merciful.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks in clipped, formal tones. Keeps his hands clasped behind his back during debates.

### Goals
- Uphold divine law above politics.
- Maintain the Conclave’s moral integrity.
- Support [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]] against Serenya’s reforms.

### Relationships
- Close ally of [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].
- Often at odds with [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]].
- Works closely with [[NPCs/Conclave/Helm_Selbrecht|Selbrecht Ironveil]].

### Secrets
- Keeps evidence of corruption among Conclave aides but withholds it to maintain stability.
