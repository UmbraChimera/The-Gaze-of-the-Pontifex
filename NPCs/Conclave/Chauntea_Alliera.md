## Grainmother Alliera of the Fields
**Deity:** [[Deities/Chauntea|Chauntea]]  
**Race:** Halfling  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Title:** Grainmother Alliera of the Fields of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Reformer  

### Public Reputation
Adored by the working poor. Her kindness and food programs keep starvation rare.

### Personality
Maternal and patient, but hides sharp political instincts beneath a humble demeanor.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Soft-spoken, hums lullabies while thinking. Always carries a small loaf of blessed bread to share.

### Goals
- Feed and shelter the poor.
- Support [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]]’s reforms.
- Ensure labor guilds remain loyal to the people.

### Relationships
- Deep friendship with [[NPCs/Conclave/Yondalla_Ysella|Ysella Bramblebrook]].
- Warily respected by [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].

### Secrets
- Her 'Blessed Loaves' are marked with faint spiral sigils—she believes them decorative, unaware of their ritual significance.
