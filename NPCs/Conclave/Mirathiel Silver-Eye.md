## Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye
**Deity:** [[Selûne|Selûne]]  
**Race:** Moon Elf  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Title:** Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Reformer  

### Public Reputation
Regarded as a holy visionary. Pilgrims gather to hear her prophecies, though few understand them.

### Personality
Dreamy and serene. Lives half in waking, half in visions.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks softly, eyes unfocused. Uses metaphors about tides and stars.

### Goals
- Spread compassion and hope through faith.
- Support reform without bloodshed.
- Prevent fanaticism within the Conclave.

### Relationships
- Loyal to [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]].
- Has cryptic respect for [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].

### Secrets
- Her prophecies sometimes include imagery of black spirals she cannot interpret.
