## Forgefather Baelrik Anvilthane
**Deity:** [[Deities/Moradin|Moradin]]  
**Race:** Dwarf  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Title:** Forgefather Baelrik Anvilthane of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Traditionalist  

### Public Reputation
Respected as the father of Hammergate industry. Workers adore him for his fairness.

### Personality
Methodical and wise. Believes honest labor sanctifies the soul.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Deep gravelly voice. Smells of smoke and oil. Pauses often to think before speaking.

### Goals
- Protect Hammergate’s craftsmen.
- Keep industry under church oversight.
- Prevent overreliance on rail technology.

### Relationships
- Ally of [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].
- Occasional mentor to [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]].

### Secrets
- Fears the heat of his forges resonates oddly beneath the city’s foundations.
