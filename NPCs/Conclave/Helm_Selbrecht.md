## High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil
**Deity:** [[Deities/Helm|Helm]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Title:** High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil of [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Traditionalist  

### Public Reputation
Feared and respected as the city’s unblinking eye. Citizens call him 'The Vigil' behind his back.

### Personality
Unyielding and pragmatic. Values order over compassion.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Quiet, deliberate speech; blindfolded eyes turn toward whoever he addresses as if truly seeing them.

### Goals
- Maintain order within [[Cities/Respite|Respite]].
- Protect the Conclave from internal threats.
- Strengthen the Watchers Eternal.

### Relationships
- Trusted by [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]].
- Tolerates [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]] but doubts her motives.

### Secrets
- Secretly maintains dossiers on all Conclave members, including the Pontifex.
