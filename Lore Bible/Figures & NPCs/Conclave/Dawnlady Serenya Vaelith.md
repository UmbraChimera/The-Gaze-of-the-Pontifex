## Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith
**Deity:** [[Lathander|Lathander]]  
**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good (publicly)  
**Title:** Dawnlady of Renewal  
**Bloc:** Reformer  

### Public Reputation
Beloved as the "Heart of the People." Her kindness and charisma make her the most recognizable face in Respite—every citizen claims to have seen her heal, feed, or console someone.

### Personality
Warm, generous, and fiercely idealistic. Publicly humble but privately calculating.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Gentle tone, perfect poise, hands always open or reaching toward others. Never speaks above a calm conversational level.

### Goals
- Expand her charities and public works.  
- Gain majority Reformer support in the Conclave.  
- Position herself as the only moral alternative to Thalanil.  

### Relationships
- Loved by [[Alaric Dathen|Sir Alaric Dathen]].  
- Opposed by [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]].  
- Supported by [[Grainmother Alliera|Grainmother Alliera]] and [[Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye|Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye]].  

### Secrets
- Secretly the high priestess of [[Tharizdun|Tharizdun]].  
- Her charities form a ritual web beneath the city to unseal the Spire.
