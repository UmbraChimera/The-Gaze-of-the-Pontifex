## High Radiance Selarra Flamehair
**Deity:** [[Sune|Sune]]  
**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Title:** High Radiance Selarra Flamehair of [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Reformer  

### Public Reputation
Adored for her beauty and charm. Critics see her as vain but harmless.

### Personality
Vivacious and passionate. Loves spectacle and attention.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks musically, laughs often, gestures dramatically.

### Goals
- Promote art and beauty.
- Strengthen Sune’s influence through pageantry.
- Support Reformers through soft power.

### Relationships
- Flirts with [[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral]].
- Allies with [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Serenya]].

### Secrets
- Keeps a mirror blessed by [[Sune|Sune]] that sometimes reflects events not yet happened.
