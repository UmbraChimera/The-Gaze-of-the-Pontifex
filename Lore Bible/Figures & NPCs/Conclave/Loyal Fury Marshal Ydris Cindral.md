## Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral
**Deity:** [[Torm|Torm]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Title:** Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral of [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Bloc:** Traditionalist  

### Public Reputation
Beloved by the Sanctified Guard for his courage. Viewed as honorable but sometimes naive.

### Personality
Loyal to a fault, deeply believes oaths bind the soul. Fierce sense of duty.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Boisterous voice, greets everyone with a firm handshake and direct eye contact.

### Goals
- Protect Respite’s unity.
- Strengthen the Sanctified Guard’s authority.
- Promote alliance between Helmites and Tormites.

### Relationships
- Respects [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]] but dislikes his elitism.
- Friend to [[High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil|High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil]].

### Secrets
- Believes he once received a personal vision from [[Torm|Torm]], but fears it was mere exhaustion.
