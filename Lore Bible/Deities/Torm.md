# 🛡️ Torm — The Loyal Fury
Patron of duty, courage, and self-sacrifice. In **Respite**, his church supplies the Sanctified Guard’s officers and exemplifies faithful service to the Pontifex.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Domains:** War, Order  
- **Symbol:** White right gauntlet  
- **Colors:** White and gold  
- **Seat on the [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Loyal Furies  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “A Tormite never breaks the line.”  
Respite’s citizens see them as soldiers of faith—dutiful and honorable, if occasionally overzealous.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Duty above self.  
- Courage in all things.  
- Loyalty to the righteous and resistance to tyranny.  
- Faith is the shield of civilization.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Brother/Sister of Service*, *Marshal*, *High Marshal*.  
- **Structure:** Military, organized in companies.  
- **Duties:** Train guards, protect embassies, and enforce oaths.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Armored gauntlet embossed with a white handprint on the left pauldron.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Partners with [[High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil|Helm’s Watchers]]; occasionally conflict with [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]]’s aloof authority.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Oath of the Gauntlet:** Knights swear lifelong vows of service.  
- **Rite of Remembrance:** Honoring those who fell in defense of the city.  
- **Festival of the Gauntlet:** Public parades of the Loyal Furies during the summer solstice.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hall of the Gauntlet:** Fortress-temple adjoining the Sanctified Guard barracks.  
- **Embassy Watchtower:** Shared chapel with Helm overlooking the foreign quarter.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral]] — Bloc: Traditionalist.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Supports discipline and Pontifex authority.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Endorses construction for civic defense; dislikes civilian rail expansion.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Torm’s clergy are the heart of Respite’s armed faith; use them to depict noble service and moral dilemmas about obedience.  
- They’re ideal allies—or obstacles—for PCs working with the Guard.
