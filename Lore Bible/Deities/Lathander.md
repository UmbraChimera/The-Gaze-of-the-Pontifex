# ☀️ Lathander — The Morninglord
God of renewal, creativity, and the dawn. In **Respite**, his faith is the banner of reform and compassion, championed by the beloved half-elf [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith]].

- **Alignment:** Neutral Good  
- **Domains:** Life, Light  
- **Symbol:** Sunrise over water  
- **Colors:** Gold, rose, and white  
- **Seat on the [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** Serenya Vaelith  
- **Primary Order:** The Dawnbringers  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “The sun rises for all.”  
Lathanderites are praised as healers and social reformers but criticized for reckless optimism.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Every dawn is a chance to begin again.  
- Aid those in darkness; spread renewal.  
- Beauty and change are divine.  
- Despair is sin—hope is duty.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Dawnbringer*, *Morninglord*, *Dawnlady*.  
- **Structure:** Decentralized; focused on outreach.  
- **Duties:** Operate the Halls of Relief, orphanages, and hospitals.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Gold-trimmed white robes with a pink-rose mantle; left arm bare or wrapped in silk bands to “welcome the dawn.”  
- **Allies & Friction:** Works with [[Grainmother Alliera|Chauntea’s]] clergy; opposed by [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]].  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Morning Glory:** Daily sunrise prayers.  
- **Blessing of the Hearth:** Simple ritual to sanctify homes.  
- **Festival of Renewal:** Massive public feast each spring—prime for Serenya’s ritual manipulation.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Golden Portico:** Soup line and clinic in the Garden of the Gods.  
- **Dawnspire Chapel:** Small shrine near the Spirewalk.  
- **The Morning Hearth:** Central orphanage and alms house.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith]] — Bloc: Reformer.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Progressive; supports democratizing temple authority.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Supports modernization as a tool of mercy and connection.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Lathander’s clergy embody hope and renewal—but unchecked optimism hides Serenya’s darker purpose.  
- Use their charities and orphanages as early signs of Tharizdun’s ritual network.
