# 🌙 Selûne — The Moonmaiden
Goddess of the moon, stars, and navigation.  
In **Respite**, Selûne’s faithful maintain night shrines and offer solace to wanderers and outcasts.

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
- **Domains:** Knowledge, Life  
- **Symbol:** Pair of eyes surrounded by seven stars  
- **Colors:** Silver, pale blue, white  
- **Seat on the [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye|Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Silver Shields  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “The Moonmaiden guides lost hearts.”  
Her temples glow softly at night; her followers are seen as kind but otherworldly.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Embrace change as the moon waxes and wanes.  
- Seek truth in the light of reflection.  
- Protect travelers and those in darkness.  
- Balance light and shadow within the soul.

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Moonhand*, *Starseer*, *Moonmaiden*.  
- **Structure:** Loose order; visions guide decisions.  
- **Duties:** Maintain waylamps, healers of the night, mediators for dream disputes.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Flowing moon-silver robes; soft lantern carried at all times.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Supports [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Serenya]]’s reforms; occasionally counters [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]]’s rigidity.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Rite of the Full Moon:** Communal prayer on rooftops or courtyards.  
- **Dream Vigil:** Overnight meditation for insight.  
- **Festival of the Moon’s Embrace:** Night of dancing, prophecy, and reconciliation.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **The Mooncourt:** Amphitheater-temple where judgments are made under moonlight.  
- **Silverlight Oratory:** Small shrine at the Spirewalk crossing.  
- **Riverside Lanterns:** Floating candles released each month.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye|Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye]] — Bloc: Reformer.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Supports peace and reform; symbolic of emotional guidance.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Opposes pollution; promotes beauty and balance.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Selûne’s faith for moments of quiet reflection or foreshadowing through dreams.  
- Their moonlit ceremonies can deliver visions or clues to the deeper mysteries beneath Respite.
