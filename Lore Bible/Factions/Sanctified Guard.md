# ⚔️ The Sanctified Guard
Unified Guard of [[Respite|Respite]]. Ever-present as watch and defenders.  
- Silver-blue armor with the [[The Spire|The Spire]] sigil on the chest.  
- The **left arm and shoulder** display a unique insignia and design reflecting each deity’s domain and aesthetic.  
- All Guards obey the [[Pontifex of Respite|Pontifex of Respite]], unless orders directly violate their deity’s creed.  

---

## ⚔️ Sanctified Guard — Arm of Faith

### Uniform
- **Standard Issue:** Silver-blue armor with tabard marked by the Spire sigil.  
- **Left Arm & Shoulder:** Customized to show deity’s style and blessings.

---

### Law & Order Powers
- **[[Justiciar Jorven Harth|Tyr — The Justicars of Tyr]]:** Full metal gauntlet and pauldrons, engraved with scales of justice. Symbolizes unyielding law.  
- **[[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Torm — The Loyal Furies]]:** Armored, polished with oath-runes etched into the vambrace, glowing faintly when oaths are spoken.  
- **[[High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil|Helm — The Watchers Eternal]]:** Heavy plate with an inset eye motif, always unblinking. Some wear crested helms tied into the shoulder design.  

---

### Nature & Hearth
- **[[Grainmother Alliera|Chauntea — The Grainwardens]]:** Simple leather sleeve with woven vine or wheat motifs, sometimes sprouting with living greenery.  
- **[[Warden Tharil Mossheart|Silvanus — The Verdant Wardens]]:** Wispy barklike armor or bare arm painted with druidic spirals, wrapped in vines.  
- **[[High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook|Yondalla — The Hearthguard]]:** Modest chain or leather with halfling hearth motifs (family knotwork, simple carvings). Friendly, unthreatening.  
- **[[Forgefather Baelrik Anvilthane|Moradin — The Forgeguard]]:** Thick, blackened steel vambrace, often with hammer-and-anvil reliefs. Some wear runed bracers forged in their home halls.  

---

### Magic & Mystical
- **[[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis|Mystra — The Spellwardens]]:** Wispy runic sleeve of chain or cloth that shimmers as though made of starlight. Glyphs pulse faintly when near magic.  
- **[[Loremaster Daelen Quillborn|Oghma — The Loreguard]]:** Scroll-leather bracer inscribed with script. Some carry quills or parchment cases affixed to the pauldron.  
- **[[Lady Mirathiel Silver-Eye|Selûne — The Silver Shields]]:** Moon-silver cloth sleeve, luminous under moonlight. Often flows like silk rather than rigid armor.  

---

### Passion, War & Death
- **[[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear|Tempus — The Battleborn]]:** Brutal, scarred steel with spiked vambrace. Shoulder often dented or decorated with battle trophies.  
- **[[High Radiance Selarra Flamehair|Sune — The Roseguard]]:** Bare arm or silken sleeve, painted with roses or flame designs. Designed to dazzle, not protect.  
- **[[Gravewarden Caleth Veynar|Kelemvor — The Gravewardens]]:** Blackened steel, etched with skeletal or chain motifs. Cold, solemn, intimidating.  

---

### Contentious Outsider
- **[[High Fist Korven Dreadmark|Bane — The Fists of Bane]]:** Heavy spiked gauntlet and pauldron, painted black-green. Brutal and fearsome — meant to instill fear, not unity.

## ⚖️ Takeaways
- **Unified look = one Guard, one city.**  
- **Left Arm/Shoulder = divine allegiance.**  
- Instantly recognizable: “That’s a Justicar of Tyr” vs “That’s a Roseguard of Sune.”  
- Subtle cultural clues: Justicars march in perfect formation; Roseguards dazzle; Grainwardens smell faintly of earth.  
- Despite differences, all stand as the shield of [[Respite|Respite]] and the will of the [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave]].
