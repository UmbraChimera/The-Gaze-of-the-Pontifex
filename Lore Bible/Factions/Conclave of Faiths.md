# 🏛️ The Conclave of Faiths
The **Conclave of Faiths** is the ruling council of archpriests who govern [[Respite]] in the name of the gods.  
Each seat represents one of the sixteen major deities recognized by the city. Together, they elect the [[Pontifex of Respite]], draft divine law, and maintain harmony among the temples.  

> **Common Saying:** “Sixteen voices, one divine will — when they agree.”

The Conclave meets within the **Hall of Concord**, at the base of the [[The Spire]], directly beneath the Pontifex’s throne chamber. Every decision shapes faith, commerce, and politics throughout the city-state.

---

## Overview
- **Members:** 16 high clerics, each the spiritual head of their deity’s temple within Respite.  
- **Presiding Officer:** The [[Pontifex of Respite]] — currently [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]].  
- **Meeting Place:** The Hall of Concord, within the Crown of Faith district.  
- **Voting Procedure:** Each seat has one vote; a two-thirds majority enacts or overturns an Edict.  
- **Power:** The Conclave’s decisions are binding divine law across all faiths within the city-state.  

---

## Political Blocs
- **Traditionalists (Order, Hierarchy, Beauty, Law):** [[Corellon Larethian]], [[Tyr]], [[Torm]], [[Helm]], [[Moradin]]  
- **Reformers (Renewal, Mercy, Compassion):** [[Lathander]], [[Chauntea]], [[Selûne]], [[Sune]], [[Yondalla]]  
- **Neutrals (Knowledge, Balance, the Weave):** [[Mystra]], [[Oghma]], [[Silvanus]]  
- **Wildcards (War, Death):** [[Tempus]], [[Kelemvor]]  
- **Outsider (Tyranny):** [[Bane]]  

Bloc loyalties shift during crises, and alliances often form around temporary interests rather than doctrine.

## Function & Governance
Each member governs their deity’s temple, clergy, and economic spheres within Respite. Together, they form a web of faith and bureaucracy that controls every aspect of life—charity, industry, education, and defense.

- **Conclave Sessions:** Held every seventh day in the Hall of Concord; open to observers but sealed during votes.  
- **Private Councils:** Factions often meet secretly in temple basements or embassy salons to negotiate trade-offs.  
- **Proxy Clerics:** Each archpriest commands a small council of advisors who manage daily operations during sessions.  
- **Voting Records:** Preserved by [[Oghma]]’s Loreguard; edits or omissions are considered heresy.

---

## Current Political Climate
- The **Traditionalists**, led by [[Corellon Larethian]] and [[Tyr]], resist industrial change and reform.  
- The **Reformers**, inspired by [[Lathander]] and [[Chauntea]], push for equality, charity, and modernization.  
- **Mystra** and **Oghma** maintain fragile neutrality to prevent schism.  
- **Tempus** and **Kelemvor** swing votes based on stability and preparedness.  
- **Bane**’s seat acts as a threat—a lawful evil balance ensuring neither side grows too idealistic.  

The next major debate: whether to extend rail lines beneath the [[The Spire]] for pilgrim transport—a decision that could literally unseal the divine lock.

---

## Influence Beyond Faith
- **Law & Justice:** [[Tyr]]’s Justicars administer courts, often enforced by [[Helm]]’s Watchers.  
- **Defense & Military:** [[Torm]]’s Loyal Furies and [[Tempus]]’s Battleborn form the Guard’s core.  
- **Charity & Relief:** [[Lathander]], [[Chauntea]], and [[Yondalla]] control aid distribution.  
- **Industry:** [[Moradin]] manages Hammergate’s economy.  
- **Magic & Knowledge:** [[Mystra]] and [[Oghma]] dictate spell use and recordkeeping.  
- **Culture & Society:** [[Corellon Larethian]] and [[Sune]] shape art and public life.  
- **Nature & Balance:** [[Silvanus]] ensures ecology tempers greed.  
- **Death & Control:** [[Kelemvor]] and [[Bane]] maintain fear and obedience in their own ways.

## Notes for Play (GM)
- The Conclave is both parliament and pantheon—every vote a sermon, every law a scripture.  
- Each seat is a potential ally, antagonist, or quest-giver.  
- Alliances shift constantly: promises, secrets, and miracles buy loyalty.  
- A Pontifex election or a Conclave schism can serve as a multi-session campaign arc.
