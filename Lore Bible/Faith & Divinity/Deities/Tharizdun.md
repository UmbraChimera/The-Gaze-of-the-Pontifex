# 🔗 Tharizdun — The Chained God
Elder evil of entropy, madness, and annihilation.  
Once imprisoned by the gods beneath the **[[Spire|Spire]]**, Tharizdun’s name is now spoken only by cults that seek to unmake creation.  
In **Respite**, his shadow moves through acts of charity and reform.

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
- **Domains:** Trickery, Death  
- **Symbol:** Black spiral  
- **Colors:** Violet-black and gray  
- **Worship:** Suppressed and forgotten—save by [[Serenya Vaelith]].  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** None. Tharizdun is erased from every archive, his existence denied by all official temples.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
*(Hidden and heretical; known only to cultists.)*  
- All creation is suffering; end it.  
- Destruction is mercy.  
- Order and chaos are illusions before oblivion.  
- The Chained God will unmake all bindings.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Prophet of the Spiral*, *Binder-Breaker*, *Whispered Voice*.  
- **Structure:** Fragmented cult hidden within legitimate charities.  
- **Duties:** Construct sigils and geometric patterns across the city to unseal the divine lock.  
- **Uniform Cue:** None publicly; hidden spirals embroidered inside robes of [[Lathander]]’s clergy.  
- **Known Agents:** [[Serenya Vaelith]], the Chained Prophet.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **The Rite of the Spiral:** Citywide ritual using geometry and blood sacrifice to open the lock.  
- **The Final Silence:** Supposed end-state where all existence ceases.  
- **False Dawn:** Masquerades as blessings of renewal in [[Lathander]]’s faith.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- None public. Cult altars hide within orphanages and soup kitchens of the Halls of Relief.  
- The true prison lies deep beneath the Spire—a labyrinthine vault known as *The Annulus.*  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- No official seat on the Conclave; infiltrates through [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith]].  
- His unseen influence bends Reformers toward entropy disguised as mercy.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Tharizdun’s worship manifests through symbols, art, and resonance—never open devotion.  
- Use him to reveal the tragic irony of Respite: its greatest miracle is the cage of its doom.
