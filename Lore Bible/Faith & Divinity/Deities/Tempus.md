# ⚔️ Tempus — The Lord of Battles
God of war, strategy, and valor.  
In **Respite**, his warriors form the backbone of the city’s defense and riot control divisions.

- **Alignment:** True Neutral  
- **Domains:** War  
- **Symbol:** Flaming sword  
- **Colors:** Crimson and black  
- **Seat on the [[Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear|Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Battleborn  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “Better the war in training than in blood.”  
The Battleborn are admired for their bravery and distrusted for their love of conflict.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- War is inevitable; face it with honor.  
- Courage in battle is virtue.  
- Strength through conflict ensures peace.  
- Cowardice is the only sin.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Warrior-Priest*, *Captain*, *Battlelord*.  
- **Structure:** Military; ranks earned through duels and merit.  
- **Duties:** Train soldiers, plan city defense, manage riot containment.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Red cloaks trimmed in black; vambraces scarred by choice.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Loyal to [[Loyal Fury Marshal Ydris Cindral|Torm’s]] discipline; clashes with [[High Radiance Selarra Flamehair|Sune’s]] pacifist reforms.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **The Clash:** Morning sparring ritual among priests.  
- **Oath of Iron:** New recruits swear fealty by touching heated steel.  
- **Festival of Blades:** Annual games celebrating valor, drawing huge crowds.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hall of the Crimson Sword:** Arena-temple for training and tournaments.  
- **Defender’s Plaza:** Open-air court for duels and oaths.  
- **Warden’s Bastion:** Garrison shared with the [[Sanctified Guard]].  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear|Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear]] — Bloc: Wildcard.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Supports whichever side promises readiness and strength.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Favors weapons innovation but rejects automation.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Tempus’s clergy as martial flavor and tension: the pragmatic soldiers amid holy idealists.  
- Their festivals can set scenes for duels, alliances, or street riots.
