## Coronal Thalanil Aurelion
**Deity:** [[Corellon Larethian|Corellon Larethian]]  
**Race:** Eladrin  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Title:** Coronal, Pontifex of [[Respite|Respite]]  
**Bloc:** Traditionalist  

### Public Reputation
Respected as the pinnacle of elven grace and divine order. The people view him as infallible but distant—his perfection feels more like marble than warmth.

### Personality
Elegant, disciplined, and utterly inflexible. Believes beauty, structure, and artistry are the truest forms of divine service.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Speaks slowly, with deliberate precision. Rarely smiles; when angered, his eyes frost with the colors of winter.

### Goals
- Preserve elven cultural dominance within the Conclave.  
- Halt industrialization and resist the rail expansion.  
- Groom a loyal successor who will maintain tradition.

### Relationships
- Rival of [[Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith|Dawnlady Serenya Vaelith]].  
- Leans on [[Justiciar Jorven Harth|Justiciar Jorven Harth]] for legal backing.  
- Distrusts [[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis|Magister Pelbranth]]'s neutrality.  

### Secrets
- Uses subtle enchantments to sway Conclave votes.  
- Hides growing cracks in the Spire’s divine wards to avoid panic.
