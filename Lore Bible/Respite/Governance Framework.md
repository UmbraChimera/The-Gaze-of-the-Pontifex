# 🏛️ Respite Governance Framework
Every faction, guild, and civic group in **[[Respite]]** ultimately answers to a higher power.  
The city’s theocracy ensures that **faith defines authority**—no guild or order operates without divine oversight.  

> **Common Saying:** “All work is worship; all law is divine.”

---

## ⚙️ The Three Layers of Governance
1. **Faction Level:** Everyday leadership—guild masters, union heads, and company boards that run local affairs.  
2. **Initial Governing Body:** The first level of justice—internal tribunals, trade councils, or city patrols that arbitrate routine disputes.  
3. **Temple Escalation:** When cases surpass mortal reach, disputes ascend to the **temple** of the appropriate deity represented in the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
   - Escalations are both civic and spiritual trials.  
   - Verdicts rendered at this level are considered divine law.

## 🛠️ Crafts & Industry
The backbone of Respite’s economy, centered in [[Hammergate]].  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Hammergate Unionists** | Union councils | [[Moradin]] | Dwarven-led labor alliance. Negotiates fair wages but resists automation; seen as too loyal to Moradin’s Forgeguard. |
| **Glasswrights’ Guild** | Craft tribunal | [[Mystra]] | Crafts arcane glass, focusing lenses for temples and laboratories; their production indirectly fuels the Spire’s resonance network. |
| **Masons’ Guild** | Masons’ court | [[Moradin]] | Builds city fortifications; rumored to have rediscovered lost runic blueprints from the Spire’s base. |

> **GM Use:** Industry disputes are prime adventure hooks—sabotage, missing plans, or heretical machinery hidden within factory blueprints.

## 💰 Commerce & Finance
Trade is the city’s bloodstream, monitored through temple-sanctioned contracts.  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Aurelion Guild Compact** | Merchant tribunal | [[Oghma]] | Manages markets and tariffs. Records every transaction—knowledge is profit. Oghma’s scribes ensure no deal is unwritten. |
| **Moneylenders’ Guild** | Lenders’ board | [[Tyr]] | Upholds fair-debt law. Ruthless in collection, but corruption rumors persist; Justiciars monitor closely. |
| **Dockworkers’ Guild** | Harbor tribunal | [[Helm]] | Oversees River Aurelion trade. Known for smugglers and secret Banite enforcers. The Watchers Eternal patrol their piers nightly. |

> **GM Use:** Corruption in these guilds can spark economic crises or moral dilemmas for PCs tied to faith, fairness, or greed.

## 🌾 Food & Agriculture
Faith in the harvest sustains both the body and the soul.  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Bakers’ League** | Oven tribunals | [[Chauntea]] | Regulates grain prices, purity laws, and festival bread. “A loaf blessed by the Mother never molds.” |
| **Butchers’ Guild** | Butchers’ hall | [[Kelemvor]] | Oversees ethical slaughter and funerary animal rites. Kelemvor’s priests monitor for signs of disease and blasphemy. |
| **Fishmongers’ Guild** | Market court | [[Silvanus]] | Manages river harvest quotas. Druidic overseers prevent overfishing; tensions rise during droughts. |

> **GM Use:** Strikes or shortages here ripple citywide—food insecurity leads to pilgrim unrest or divine intervention.

## 🎭 Arts & Culture
Faith through expression. The temples of [[Sune]] and [[Corellon Larethian]] dominate cultural life.  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Actors’ Guild** | Troupe tribunal | [[Sune]] | Stages moral parables and romantic dramas; often accused of pushing Reformist propaganda. |
| **Painters’ & Sculptors’ Guild** | Studio court | [[Corellon Larethian]] | Sponsors murals, statuary, and public beauty projects. Rumors say their frescoes hide divine runic patterns. |

> **GM Use:** Art as intrigue—patrons commission “holy art” that may also conceal prophecy or forbidden geometry.

## 🕵️ Crime & Shadow
Every city breeds corruption; even faith cannot banish it entirely.  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Lantern Syndicate** | City Watch | [[Helm]] | Smugglers and fences who operate in the Pilgrims’ Quarter. “The Watch sees, but gold blinds the eye.” |
| **Black Scriptors** | Watch/scribes | [[Mystra]] / [[Tyr]] | Copy forbidden texts and arcane heresies. Caught scribes face magical erasure or divine trial. |
| **Street Gangs** | Watch patrols | [[Helm]] | Controlled chaos tolerated by the Watch. Occasionally conscripted into Guard service during uprisings. |

> **GM Use:** The Lantern Syndicate connects to Banite corruption and Serenya’s charities—perfect for investigative or noir-style arcs.

## Notes for Play (GM)
- The divine bureaucracy gives structure to every story: a theft, trial, or miracle always escalates to faith.  
- Use guild disputes as inciting incidents for intrigue—each reflects its temple’s ethos.  
- The multi-layered governance means no decision is purely civic; **every law is divine politics.**
