# 🕊️ Act 1 — The Dawn Rally
*An opening act of faith, fire, and fracture.*

---

## Introduction
The holy city-state of **[[Respite]]** gleams beneath the gaze of sixteen gods.  
For centuries, the [[Conclave of Faiths]] has ruled in harmony under the [[Pontifex of Respite]], mortal vessel of divine unity.  
But harmony erodes: the forges of [[Hammergate]] cool at dusk, the curfews grow longer, and the [[Sanctified Guard]] watches every prayer.  

Into this unrest rises **[[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]**, half-elven priestess of [[Sarenrae]], beloved for her mercy and tireless charities.  
On the first morning of Brightmarch she will speak before the [[The Spire]]—and that speech will change Respite forever.

---

## Running This Act
The rally is the campaign’s **inciting event**.  
Characters begin at 5th level and should finish the act near 6th or 7th.

**Themes**
- *Faith vs Authority* — When law is divine, can mercy rebel?  
- *Hope vs Control* — Can compassion survive theocracy?  
- *Truth vs Belief* — What if miracles are manufactured?  

**Tone**
Political-religious tension, civic heroism, and street-level chaos.  
Think incense and gunpowder, sermons over shouting crowds, sanctity colliding with industry.

---

## Story Overview
- **Opening Scene:** PCs attend or guard the public sermon later known as *The Dawn Rally*.  
- **Inciting Incident:** Serenya is shot mid-speech.  
- **Chaos:** Panic, riot, Guard lockdown.  
- **Aftermath:** She survives—declared a living saint.  
- **Consequence:** Curfews, inquisitions, and factional divides deepen.  

By the end of the act the party must decide where their loyalties lie… or have that decision made for them.

## Marks of the City
A theocracy of contradictions—sacred and industrial, radiant and rusting.

- **Sight:** Steam beneath stained glass; banners of sixteen gods.  
- **Sound:** Bells, hammers, sermons, shouts.  
- **Smell:** Incense, forge-smoke, oil, and bread.  
- **Feel:** Order on the surface, fear underneath.  

---

## Chapter 1 — The Plaza of Concord
**Setting:** The great square at the Spire’s base, overflowing with citizens and clergy.

> *The dawn bells ring nine times. The Spire gleams like a blade of crystal light.  
> Banners of sixteen gods ripple overhead, forming a circle of color in the white sky.  
> A half-elven priestess steps onto a dais, robes blazing with Sarenrae’s sunburst.  
> The crowd chants: “Light for all!”*  

**Key NPCs**
- [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]] — delivers the speech.  
- [[Sir Alaric Dathen]] — her sworn protector.  
- Two Sarenrite clerics and two Sanctified soldiers.  
- [[High Justiciar Arria Varus]] — overseeing security for the [[Sanctified Guard]].

**Player Hooks**
- *The Guard:* on duty under the Blades of Valor.  
- *The Faithful:* followers inspired by Serenya’s sermons.  
- *The Scholar:* documenting the speech.  
- *The Laborer:* Hammergate worker drawn by hope.  
- *The Outsider:* passing pilgrim caught in history.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Speech
Serenya’s oration burns with compassion and defiance.  
PCs may notice unease among clergy and guards.

**Moments**
- Insight DC 13 — She has practiced this speech many times; emotion perfectly measured.  
- Perception DC 15 — A glint of metal on a rooftop. Too late.  

> *A rifle cracks. Serenya jerks, crimson blooming across her sun-emblazoned robes.  
> She gasps mid-word and falls into Alaric’s arms as bells erupt across the city.*  

---

## Chapter 3 — The Seal of Silence
The [[Sanctified Guard]] surges forward, shields locking into a wall of silver-blue steel.  
Crowds scatter. Panic spirals.

**Possible Actions**
- *Rescue Civilians:* Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13.  
- *Aid Serenya:* Medicine or Heal DC 15; wound glows gold.  
- *Pursue Shooter:* Perception DC 17; trail ends in chaos.  
- *Calm Mob:* Diplomacy DC 14; failure sparks riot.  

**GM Note:** The shooter dies unseen. The spectacle — not the kill — was the purpose.  
When order returns, the Guard enacts the **Seal of Silence**: witnesses detained “for faith’s protection.”

---

## Chapter 4 — The Hall of Concord
The PCs face inquiry beneath the Spire.  
[[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] demands truth; Reformers demand leniency.

**Events**
- [[Sir Alaric Dathen]] thanks the PCs and pleads for aid.  
- [[High Justiciar Arria Varus]] begins a formal investigation.  
- PCs navigate politics, forming early alliances.

**Faction Seeds**
- **Reformers:** Serenya’s allies seek testimony.  
- **Traditionalists:** recruit the PCs as informants.  
- **The Guard:** request impartial witnesses.  

---

## Chapter 5 — The City at Dusk
By sundown, curfews choke the streets.  
Sermons harden, rumors ignite.  
The PCs—now public witnesses—become symbols in a faith war.

> *“The bells no longer ring for prayer,” mutters a vendor. “They ring to warn us.”*

From here the story diverges: investigation, faith, or conspiracy.

## Adventure Hooks (for PCs)
1. **The Faithful:** Follower of Serenya; her survival is divine proof.  
2. **The Guard:** Ordered to protect the rally; now burdened by guilt.  
3. **The Laborer:** Hammergate worker inspired by her promised reforms.  
4. **The Scholar:** Witness hired to transcribe; notes missing words.  
5. **The Outsider:** Pilgrim trapped by curfew and prophecy.  

---

## Closing of Act 1
The assassination shatters Respite’s illusion of unity.  
The [[Conclave of Faiths]] fractures, whispers of rebellion spread, and the PCs stand at the center.  

Serenya becomes the saint the city never asked for — and her shadow stretches toward the Spire.  
The Pontifex’s gaze now falls upon the PCs.

> **End of Act 1 — “The Dawn Rally.”**
