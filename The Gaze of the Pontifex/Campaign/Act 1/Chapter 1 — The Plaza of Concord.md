# 🕊️ Chapter 1 — The Plaza of Concord
*A thousand prayers rise — and one shot silences them.*

---

## Scene Overview
The PCs arrive in the **Plaza of Concord**, the sacred amphitheater at the foot of [[The Spire]].  
The day is Brightmarch 1st, the Feast of the Covenant — banners of sixteen gods snap in the morning wind, and Respite holds its breath for what promises to be a historic sermon.

Serenya Vaelith’s speech is meant to call for mercy, reform, and unity among the city’s faithful.  
Instead, it will ignite a crisis that shatters divine order.

---

## Setting the Scene
**Time:** Early morning  
**Weather:** Warm sunlight through high clouds, breeze heavy with incense and coal-smoke  
**Soundtrack:** Bells, chanting, the low hum of the Spire

> *The dawn bells ring nine times. The Spire gleams like a blade of crystal light.  
> Banners of sixteen gods ripple overhead, forming a circle of color above the marble plaza.  
> Vendors shout blessings between hymn verses, and the crowd swells toward the central dais where a golden-haired priestess steps forward.*

## Arrival Hooks
- **Guard Detail:** The PCs serve under Arria Varus’s command, assigned to crowd control or rooftop watch.  
- **Faithful Attendees:** They came to hear Serenya’s call for mercy, perhaps already sympathetic to her Reformers.  
- **Guild Envoys:** Sent from [[Hammergate]] or the [[Aurelion Guild Compact]] to gauge whether reforms threaten their profits.  
- **Scholars:** Chroniclers from the [[Scholarium]] recording the sermon for posterity.  
- **Pilgrims or Outsiders:** Seeking blessing from the Dawnmother; witnesses to destiny.

---

## Pre-Speech Events (Free Role-Play)
**1. Crowd Tension:**  
The PCs can sense opposing emotions in the crowd — joy, anxiety, fear.  
- *Perception DC 14* — Notice whispering groups wearing sunburst pins inverted into spirals.  
- *Diplomacy DC 15* — Calm arguments between Reformers and Traditionalists.  
- *Religion DC 13* — Recognize conflicting prayer chants; Sarenrae’s mercy vs. Iomedae’s order.

**2. Serenya’s Procession:**  
A golden carriage bearing the sigil of the Dawnflower arrives from the [[Halls of Relief]]. She steps out barefoot, a calculated act of humility.  
- *Insight DC 14* — Her serenity feels rehearsed, her smile slightly strained.  
- *Lore (Politics) DC 15* — Attendance by multiple Conclave aides signals the Pontifex is watching closely.  

**3. Side Encounters (Optional)**  
- A beggar child tugs at a PC’s cloak, asking for “a coin to buy light.” If given alms, the child later helps the PCs navigate the riot.  
- A Black Tribunal agent in red-black robes warns: “Too many words can burn brighter than the sun.” He vanishes into the crowd.

## Serenya’s Speech
> *“Brothers and sisters of the dawn, we live in a city of light — yet how many still dwell in shadow?  
>  The Pontifex tells us stillness is safety — but peace built on silence is not peace at all.  
>  The forges of Hammergate cool, while the hungry freeze in darkness.  
>  Faith without mercy is tyranny by another name.”*

## Optional Interactions Before the Shot
If the PCs engage:  
- **Question Arria Varus:** She warns, “Hold the line. No matter what she says.”  
- **Assist Clerics:** Healing exhausted pilgrims earns early goodwill.  
- **Speak with Serenya:** A PC of faith may exchange brief words; she blesses them with *“Light finds those who seek it in darkness.”*  
- **Investigate Crowd:** Discover a pamphlet quoting “The Rough Beast’s Chains Will Break.” Knowledge (Religion) DC 17 links it to forbidden Rovagug heresy.

---

## Escalation Clock (Pre-Shot Tension)
You can track tension with a simple 1–5 scale:  

1️⃣ Excitement — Hopeful crowd; peaceful.  
2️⃣ Unease — Arguments break out.  
3️⃣ Hostility — Guards push back hecklers.  
4️⃣ Imminent Crisis — Shouts of “Heretic!” and “Saint!” collide.  
5️⃣ Trigger — Gunshot or spell erupts; chaos begins.

## The Shot
> *A single crack splits the air. Serenya jerks mid-sentence, clutching her abdomen as scarlet stains her sunburst.  
>  Bells scream from every tower; the crowd erupts into panic.*

## Consequences & Transition
Within minutes, the **[[Sanctified Guard]]** locks the plaza under the **Seal of Silence**.  
Witnesses are herded toward the Hall of Concord for “protective questioning.”  
Rumors ignite instantly — Reformers cry miracle, Traditionalists whisper heresy.

**If the PCs helped Serenya:** they are publicly praised by the Reformers.  
**If they aided the Guard:** they earn official standing and future favors.  
**If they fled:** they become hunted as potential agitators.

This moment cements each character’s first political alignment in Respite’s struggle.
