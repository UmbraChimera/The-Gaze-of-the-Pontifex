# 🏛️ The Conclave of Faiths
The **Conclave of Faiths** is the ruling council of archpriests who govern [[Respite]] in the name of the gods.  
Each seat represents one of the sixteen major deities recognized by the city. Together, they elect the [[Pontifex of Respite]], draft divine law, and maintain harmony among the temples.  

> **Common Saying:** “Sixteen voices, one divine will — when they agree.”

The Conclave meets within the **Hall of Concord**, at the base of [[The Spire]], directly beneath the Pontifex’s throne chamber. Every decision shapes faith, commerce, and politics throughout the city-state.

---

## Overview
- **Members:** 16 high clerics, each the spiritual head of their deity’s temple within Respite.  
- **Presiding Officer:** The [[Pontifex of Respite]] — currently [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]].  
- **Meeting Place:** The Hall of Concord, within the Crown of Faith district.  
- **Voting Procedure:** Enacting or repealing laws requires **12 of 16 votes**.  
- **Power:** The Conclave’s decisions are binding divine law across all faiths within the city-state.  

---

## Political Blocs
- **Traditionalists (Order, Hierarchy, Beauty, Law):** [[Shelyn]], [[Abadar]], [[Iomedae]], [[Torag]], [[Erastil]]  
- **Reformers (Renewal, Mercy, Freedom):** [[Sarenrae]], [[Desna]], [[Cayden Cailean]], [[Calistria]], [[Gozreh]]  
- **Neutrals (Knowledge, Balance, Death):** [[Nethys]], [[Irori]], [[Pharasma]]  
- **Wildcard (War):** [[Gorum]]  
- **Outsider (Tyranny):** [[Asmodeus]]  

Bloc loyalties shift during crises, and alliances often form around temporary interests rather than doctrine.

## Function & Governance
Each member governs their deity’s temple, clergy, and civic sphere within Respite. Together, they weave a network of faith and bureaucracy controlling charity, trade, justice, and education.

- **Conclave Sessions:** Held weekly in the Hall of Concord; open for public sermons but sealed for votes.  
- **Private Councils:** Factions hold clandestine meetings in temple crypts and embassy salons to bargain divine favor.  
- **Proxy Clerics:** Each archpriest commands sub-orders managing their districts and temples.  
- **Voting Records:** Preserved by [[Irori]]’s Loreguard; falsifying them is considered heresy of the highest order.

---

## Current Political Climate
- The **Traditionalists**, led by [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]) and [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]), preserve order, hierarchy, and divine artistry.  
- The **Reformers**, united by [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]), push for freedom, mercy, and social reform.  
- **Neutrals** like [[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis]] ([[Nethys]]) and [[Ascetic Dhenar Rajesh]] ([[Irori]]) maintain delicate balance through study and discipline.  
- [[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear]] ([[Gorum]]) acts independently, voting based on strength and circumstance.  
- [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]) represents the tolerated tyranny keeping rebellion in check.  

The Conclave stands on a knife’s edge — torn between light and law, mercy and order.  
The next great decision: whether to expand pilgrim rail lines beneath [[The Spire]], risking the unsealing of the divine prison below.

## Influence Beyond Faith
- **Law & Justice:** [[Iomedae]] and [[Abadar]] command courts and commerce.  
- **Defense & Military:** [[Torag]] and [[Gorum]] lead the [[Sanctified Guard]]’s martial wings.  
- **Charity & Healing:** [[Sarenrae]] and [[Erastil]] sustain the poor.  
- **Industry & Trade:** [[Torag]] and [[Abadar]] build and balance the city’s lifeblood.  
- **Magic & Knowledge:** [[Nethys]] and [[Irori]] guide research, archives, and education.  
- **Art & Culture:** [[Shelyn]] and [[Calistria]] define Respite’s beauty and intrigue.  
- **Nature & Balance:** [[Gozreh]] ensures harmony between city and earth.  
- **Death & Judgment:** [[Pharasma]] administers the city’s inevitable end.  
- **Fear & Order:** [[Asmodeus]] enforces stability through shadow and threat.

## Notes for Play (GM)
- The Conclave is both **parliament and pantheon** — every vote a sermon, every law a divine compromise.  
- Alliances shift constantly: one week’s ally becomes the next week’s accuser.  
- Each seat can serve as a patron, antagonist, or questgiver.  
- The sealed seat of [[Rovagug]] — denied yet ever-present — may one day demand a voice.
