# ⚔️ The Sanctified Guard
The **Sanctified Guard** is the unified holy army and peacekeeping force of [[Respite]].  
Ever-present in the city’s streets and temples, the Guard serves as both symbol and sword of divine order.  
They wear silver-blue armor bearing the [[The Spire|The Spire]] sigil across the chest, while the **left arm and shoulder** display insignia of their patron deity.  
Each division follows its god’s tenets first, the [[Pontifex of Respite]] second — unless his orders break their sacred oaths.  

> **Motto:** “Sixteen gods, one shield.”

---

## ⚔️ The Arm of Faith

### Uniform
- **Standard Issue:** Silver-blue tabard and armor bearing the Spire sigil.  
- **Left Arm & Shoulder:** Embellished with divine symbols and motifs — each order’s faith reflected in craftsmanship and ornamentation.

---

### Law & Order
- **[[High Justiciar Arria Varus\|Iomedae — The Blades of Valor]]:** Gleaming plate, etched with halos and scales. Represents unbreakable justice and honor in law.  
- **[[Archon Varrin Calistane\|Abadar — The Gilded Watch]]:** Polished gold vambrace embossed with keys and scales. Guards the city’s contracts, vaults, and courthouses.  
- **[[Prelate Korven Dreadmark\|Asmodeus — The Black Tribunal]]:** Spiked crimson steel, the gauntlet marked with infernal runes. Terrifying enforcers who impose order through fear.  

---

### Hearth & Craft
- **[[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong\|Erastil — The Hearthguard]]:** Simple leather and bronze bracers with carved knotwork of hearthfires. Community protectors and supply overseers.  
- **[[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine\|Torag — The Iron Sentinels]]:** Blackened steel sleeves engraved with hammers and mountain peaks. Engineers, smiths, and siege masters.  
- **[[Alewarden Ysella Bramblebrook\|Cayden Cailean — The Shield of Joy]]:** Polished steel paired with colored ribbons and tankard insignia. Tavern keepers turned peacekeepers, beloved by the poor.  

---

### Knowledge & Magic
- **[[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis\|Nethys — The Arcane Sentinels]]:** Enchanted vambraces that shimmer with runes of balance. Regulate and defend magical law.  
- **[[Ascetic Dhenar Rajesh\|Irori — The Order of the Scroll]]:** Linen wraps inscribed with sutras of enlightenment. Record keepers and divine archivists.  
- **[[Gravewarden Orrin Varith\|Pharasma — The Pale Sentinels]]:** Black-lacquered steel adorned with spirals of white pearl. Guardians of the necropolis and the sanctity of death.  

---

### Passion & Inspiration
- **[[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion\|Shelyn — The Petals of Honor]]:** Silk-trimmed armor with rose motifs and flowing ribbons. Ceremonial guards and diplomats of grace.  
- **[[Lustbringer Velithra Thornkiss\|Calistria — The Whispered Blades]]:** Fine chain mail with gilded stinger insignia. Operatives of intrigue and subtle enforcement.  
- **[[Starseer Mirathiel Silver-Eye\|Desna — The Silver Shields]]:** Moonlit silks that shimmer in starlight. Night watch and guardians of travelers.  

---

### War & Nature
- **[[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear\|Gorum — The Crimson Host]]:** Brutal scarred armor marked by past blows. Riot suppressors and first into every battle.  
- **[[Warden Tharo Stormleaf\|Gozreh — The Stormwardens]]:** Barksteel arm-plates intertwined with wave motifs. Rangers and elemental wardens of the River Aurelion.  
- **[[Serenya Vaelith\|Sarenrae — The Dawnwardens]]:** Gold-edged armor with sunburst engravings. Healers, mediators, and bringers of light.

## ⚖️ Takeaways
- **Unified Look, Divine Identity:** One Guard, one city — but every arm bears its god’s mark.  
- **Left Arm/Shoulder = Faith:** Instantly readable symbols of loyalty and creed.  
- **Cultural Flavor:** Petals of Honor glide with ceremony, Crimson Host marches with thunder, and Shield of Joy laughs through danger.  
- Despite ideological divides, the Sanctified Guard remains the living embodiment of Respite’s creed:  
  > *“Sixteen gods, one shield — and one heartbeat.”*
