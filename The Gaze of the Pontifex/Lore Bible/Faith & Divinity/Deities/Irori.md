# 📚 Irori — The Master of Masters
**Titles:** The Enlightened One, The Perfect Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Domains:** Knowledge, Self-Perfection  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Unarmed Strike  
**Symbol:** Open hand surrounded by sun rays  
**Sacred Animal:** Crane  

---

## Edicts
- Strive for self-perfection through discipline and study.  
- Seek mastery over body, mind, and spirit.  
- Teach others the pursuit of inner balance and wisdom.  

## Anathema
- Indulge in sloth or excess.  
- Destroy or suppress knowledge.  
- Impose your beliefs through coercion rather than example.  

---

## Irori’s Faith in Respite
Irori’s faith manifests through the **Monastery of the Perfect Quill**, a serene, white-stone structure tucked near the [[The Spire]] in the Crown of Faith district.  
His monks are archivists, philosophers, and truth-keepers — responsible for transcribing Conclave sessions and maintaining the Living Codex, a self-updating magical record of divine law.  

Politically, the Iroran clergy stand apart. They rarely vote, choosing observation over interference, yet their documentation of truth gives them subtle, immense influence.  

> *“The gods speak in silence; mortals learn to listen.”*
