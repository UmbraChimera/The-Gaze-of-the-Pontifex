# ☀️ Sarenrae — The Dawnflower
**Titles:** The Healing Flame, The Everlight, The Redeemer  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Domains:** Sun, Healing, Redemption  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Scimitar  
**Symbol:** An angelic ankh surrounded by radiant fire  
**Sacred Animal:** Dove  

---

## Edicts
- Bring light and healing to those in darkness.  
- Offer redemption before judgment.  
- Seek peace, but strike swiftly against irredeemable evil.  
- Act with compassion and courage.  

## Anathema
- Deny the possibility of redemption.  
- Strike down a foe who surrenders or repents.  
- Allow hatred to guide your heart or faith.  

---

## Sarenrae’s Faith in Respite
The **Cathedral of the Dawn** is Respite’s beacon — a towering hall of white stone crowned by golden glass domes that catch the sunrise and scatter its light across the city.  
Her faith is the heart of the Reformer movement: charity, equality, and renewal.  

Sarenrae’s followers run hospitals, orphanages, and relief kitchens throughout Respite, especially in the [[Halls of Relief]].  
They are beloved by the poor and mistrusted by the wealthy, who see their compassion as a threat to order.  

> *“Even in the longest night, the dawn waits to be kindled.”*
