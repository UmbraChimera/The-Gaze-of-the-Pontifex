# ✨ Nethys — The All-Seeing Eye
**Titles:** The Keeper of Magic, The Two-Faced God, The Archmage Divine  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Domains:** Magic, Creation, Destruction  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Staff  
**Symbol:** Black and white mask split down the center, an open eye at the brow  
**Sacred Animal:** Owl  

---

## Edicts
- Study and preserve the art of magic.  
- Use magic to expand mortal understanding.  
- Maintain balance between creation and destruction.  

## Anathema
- Suppress magical knowledge out of fear or greed.  
- Destroy magical texts or artifacts.  
- Treat the Weave of magic as mere utility rather than divine mystery.

---

## Nethys’s Faith in Respite
Nethys’s temple, **The Hall of Refraction**, towers near the base of the [[The Spire]], its architecture a shifting contradiction of mirrored glass and black stone.  
His priests — called **Magisters** — serve as magical regulators, scholars, and enforcers of arcane law.  

They hold immense political sway as keepers of the city’s most dangerous secrets: the wards protecting the Spire and the enchantments that stabilize Respite’s ever-growing infrastructure.  
Their neutrality masks deep internal schisms between those who see Nethys as creation incarnate and those who revere his destructive side.  

> *“Magic is the mind of the divine; chaos and order are its breath.”*
