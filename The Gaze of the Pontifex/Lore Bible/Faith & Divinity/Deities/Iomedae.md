# ⚔️ Iomedae — The Inheritor
**Titles:** The Inheritor, Light of the Sword  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Domains:** Honor, Valor, Justice  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Longsword  
**Symbol:** A white sword rising from a halo of gold light  
**Sacred Animal:** Lion  

---

## Edicts
- Act with honor and courage.  
- Defend the innocent and the faithful.  
- Be truthful, even when it costs you.  
- Inspire others through example.

## Anathema
- Break an oath of justice or truth.  
- Strike down a helpless foe without cause.  
- Ignore a call for aid from the defenseless.

---

## Iomedae’s Faith in Respite
The Inheritor’s temple, **The Hall of Virtues**, sits across from the [[Hall of Scales]] in the Crown of Faith district.  
Her followers form the backbone of Respite’s moral authority — paladins, judges, and sergeants of the [[Sanctified Guard]].  
Where Abadar represents law as contract, Iomedae represents law as conviction.

Within the Conclave, her priests stand as **guardians of ethics**, often voting with Abadar’s Traditionalists but placing principle above profit.

> *“Law without honor is tyranny in robes.”*

---

## The Blades of Valor
Iomedae’s order within the [[Sanctified Guard]] acts as tribunal marshals and executioners of divine sentences.  
Their oath: *“Justice burns brightest in clean hands.”*
