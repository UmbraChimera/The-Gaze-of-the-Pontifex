# 😈 Asmodeus — The Prince of Darkness
**Titles:** The Archfiend, The First Contract, The Lawgiver  
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
**Domains:** Pride, Tyranny, Slavery, Contracts  
**Divine Font:** Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Mace  
**Symbol:** Red pentagram within a circle or flaming ruby rod  
**Sacred Animal:** Serpent  

---

## Edicts
- Uphold order through fear and control.  
- Punish rebellion and lawlessness.  
- Enforce obedience through structure and consequence.  
- Manipulate chaos to maintain dominance.

## Anathema
- Show mercy to those who defy rightful rule.  
- Break a sworn contract.  
- Place compassion above order.

---

## Asmodeus’s Faith in Respite
While officially despised, the Church of Asmodeus holds one of the most politically powerful seats in the Conclave.  
Its clergy — known as **The Black Tribunal** — serve as inquisitors, contract enforcers, and divine spies for the Pontifex.  

Their public creed is simple: “Order at any cost.”  
They are both the shadow and skeleton of Respite’s theocracy — a quiet reminder that unity often comes chained.  

Their temples are hidden behind courthouse façades, and their sermons are whispers of discipline, debt, and eternal hierarchy.  

> *“Freedom is chaos. Chaos is death. Order alone endures.”*
