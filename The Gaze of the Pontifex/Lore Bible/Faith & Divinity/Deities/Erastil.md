# 🌾 Erastil — The Old Deadeye
**Titles:** The Hunter, Father of the Fields  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Domains:** Family, Farming, Community  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Longbow  
**Symbol:** Bow and arrow over sheaves of wheat  
**Sacred Animal:** Elk  

---

## Edicts
- Care for your family and community.  
- Hunt only for food or defense.  
- Protect the innocent and guide the young.  

## Anathema
- Abandon family or community duties.  
- Hunt for sport or vanity.  
- Destroy fertile land without cause.  

---

## Erastil’s Faith in Respite
Erastil’s followers form the quiet backbone of Respite’s outer districts.  
The halfling and human farmers of the **Halls of Relief** revere him as the god of hearth and harvest.  
His clergy remind the Conclave that the city’s light shines only because the farms feed its flame.

Politically, Erastil’s priests serve as the **moral conscience** of the Traditionalists — they oppose cruelty, corruption, and greed, even among their allies.

> *“A city without families is a field without roots.”*
