# ☠️ Pharasma — The Lady of Graves
**Titles:** The Gray Lady, Mother of Souls, The Fatekeeper  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Domains:** Death, Fate, Prophecy  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Dagger  
**Symbol:** Spiral shell or comet tail  
**Sacred Animal:** Crow  

---

## Edicts
- Respect death as part of life’s cycle.  
- Protect graves and funerary sites.  
- Guide souls to their proper fate.  
- Speak truthfully about mortality.  

## Anathema
- Create undead or disturb tombs.  
- Use necromancy for selfish gain.  
- Defy the natural cycle of death.

---

## Pharasma’s Faith in Respite
Pharasma’s great **Cathedral of Passing** stands beside the River Aurelion, separating Respite’s bustling districts from its vast necropolis.  
Her clergy maintain the **Scrolls of Silence**, an archive that lists every citizen’s death and burial site.  
They serve as morticians, funeral directors, and legal arbiters of wills and inheritances.  

Her faith commands respect and unease. No citizen dares offend the Gravewardens — the silent priests who ensure the dead stay buried and that souls pass uncorrupted to judgment.  

Politically, the Pharasmins are neutral but feared; every faction requires their blessing for legitimacy in death.

> *“When all lights fade, even the gods must walk the spiral.”*
