# 🛠️ Torag — The Father of Creation
**Titles:** The Forge Father, Guardian of the Anvil  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Domains:** Forge, Protection, Strategy  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Warhammer  
**Symbol:** Hammer striking an anvil over a mountain peak  
**Sacred Animal:** Ram  

---

## Edicts
- Forge with purpose and honor your craft.  
- Defend your home and those who depend on you.  
- Plan carefully and stand firm against chaos.  
- Reward loyalty and reliability.

## Anathema
- Carelessly destroy or waste crafted items.  
- Betray allies or abandon your post.  
- Neglect to prepare before battle or conflict.

---

## Torag’s Faith in Respite
Torag’s forge-temple dominates the **Hammergate** district, where divine flame and steel intermingle with the city’s new industrial machinery.  
To his followers, every hammer strike is a prayer and every forge a sanctuary.  

His clergy act as engineers, blacksmiths, and defenders of civic infrastructure.  
Politically, Torag’s priests are **the builders of order**—literal and metaphorical. They side with Abadar’s bureaucrats and Shelyn’s artisans, seeing all three as architects of civilization’s soul.

> *“Creation is duty; destruction is negligence.”*

---

## The Anvil Guard
The Toragite division of the [[Sanctified Guard]] serves as both engineers and heavy infantry. They repair, reinforce, and—when needed—demolish for divine purpose.  

Their motto: *“Steel never kneels.”*
