# 🍷 Cayden Cailean — The Drunken Hero
**Titles:** The Lucky Drunk, The Hero of the Common Folk, The Accidental God  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Domains:** Freedom, Bravery, Ale  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Rapier  
**Symbol:** Tankard emblazoned with a smiling face  
**Sacred Animal:** Dog  

---

## Edicts
- Defend freedom and good cheer wherever they are threatened.  
- Help those in need without expecting reward.  
- Stand up to tyranny, cruelty, and corruption.  
- Celebrate life — even in hardship.

## Anathema
- Enslave or oppress another creature.  
- Abandon comrades or betray friends.  
- Refuse honest hospitality.

---

## Cayden Cailean’s Faith in Respite
Cayden’s faithful embody the heart of Respite’s working class — sailors, dockhands, performers, and brewers.  
His temple, **The Hero’s Rest**, doubles as a tavern and refuge. There, hymns are songs, sermons are stories, and every mug is a holy relic of joy.  

Politically, the Caydenites are the **moral counterbalance** to Abadar’s rigid order and Asmodeus’s tyranny.  
They champion laborers’ rights, personal liberty, and laughter as sacred protest.  

> *“The gods can have their thrones — give me a good drink and a cause worth dying for.”*
