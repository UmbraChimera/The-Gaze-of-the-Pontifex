# 💃 Calistria — The Savored Sting
**Titles:** The Lady in Lace, The Savored Sting, The Unforgotten Passion  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Domains:** Lust, Revenge, Trickery  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Whip  
**Symbol:** A wasp with jewelled wings or a barbed stinger entwined in silk  
**Sacred Animal:** Wasp  

---

## Edicts
- Indulge in desire and pleasure without shame.  
- Avenge slights that harm the heart or the spirit.  
- Teach others the beauty of passion and the danger of betrayal.  

## Anathema
- Deny yourself joy or punish others for love.  
- Betray loyalty without cause or cunning.  
- Use intimacy as cruelty without art or lesson.  

---

## Calistria’s Faith in Respite
Calistria’s temple — *The House of Whispered Lace* — is both salon and sanctuary.  
Her clergy serve as diplomats, courtesans, and spies.  Within the marble halls of Respite, the Savored Sting’s followers wield seduction and wit as deftly as others wield blades.  

Politically, they are the **Reformers’ informants and illusionists**, providing soft power in a world of law and steel.  
They remind even the most righteous that the gods themselves are not immune to temptation.

> *“Every promise, every lie, every kiss — each is its own holy scripture.”*
