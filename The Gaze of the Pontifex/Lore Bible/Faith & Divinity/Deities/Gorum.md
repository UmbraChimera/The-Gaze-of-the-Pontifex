# ⚔️ Gorum — The Lord in Iron
**Titles:** Our Lord in Iron, The War God, The Armored Fury  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral  
**Domains:** Battle, Strength, Courage  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Greatsword  
**Symbol:** Iron suit of armor with sword raised  
**Sacred Animal:** Bull  

---

## Edicts
- Engage in battle; prove your strength through conflict.  
- Uphold courage and valor in war.  
- Honor strength wherever it is found, even in enemies.  

## Anathema
- Flee from a battle without cause.  
- Betray an ally on the battlefield.  
- Use cowardly tactics or deceit to win.  

---

## Gorum’s Faith in Respite
In Respite, Gorum’s temple is less a sanctuary and more a fortress.  
The **Crimson Bastion**, as it’s known, echoes with the clash of steel day and night.  
The Gorumites serve as mercenaries, duelists, and riot-breakers — holy warriors who see divine truth in every contest of might.  

They stand apart from politics, seeing the Conclave’s bickering as “a quieter kind of war.”  
Their votes shift unpredictably, swayed only by those who prove strength of conviction, not persuasion.  

> *“Peace is the pause between the last strike and the next.”*
