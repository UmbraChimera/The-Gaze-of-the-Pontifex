# 🕳️ Rovagug — The Chained God
**Titles:** The Rough Beast, The Worldbreaker, The Devourer Below  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
**Domains:** Destruction, Apocalypse, Wrath, Catastrophe  
**Divine Font:** Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Greataxe  
**Symbol:** Black spiral devouring itself or jagged maw bound by chains  
**Sacred Animal:** Centipede  

---

## Edicts
- Tear down what the gods have built.  
- Unmake the false order of creation.  
- Sow chaos and ruin until all returns to primordial silence.  

## Anathema
- Create lasting beauty or civilization.  
- Build or preserve anything permanent.  
- Protect divine law or mortal authority.

---

## Rovagug’s Faith in Respite
Officially, **Rovagug does not exist.**  
The [[Conclave of Faiths]] erased his name from all sanctioned scripture centuries ago.  
The sealed seat in the Hall of Concord — the Sixteenth that is never spoken of — is said to have once belonged to him.

In truth, his **prison lies beneath the [[The Spire]]**, sealed by the gods after the Covenant Wars.  
The entire city of Respite is built atop that divine lock — a holy scar turned monument.  
The Spire’s eternal hum is the sound of his hunger gnawing against his chains.

Among the desperate, the mad, and the disillusioned, the cult of Rovagug survives as a **whispered heresy**, known as *The Spiral*.  
Its faithful believe destruction is not evil, but **divine renewal** — that through annihilation, the world may finally rest.

> *“When all creation is ash, the silence will sing again.”*

---

## The Spiral Doctrine
The cult teaches that the Spire is a **flower of endings**, its geometry a divine lock that holds back Rovagug’s true form — the Rough Beast that devoured worlds before time.  
They await the day when the **Rite of the Spiral** completes: a ritual not of summoning, but of unbinding.  

Each act of destruction — plague, fire, war, collapse — is seen as a “petal unfolding” in the Spire’s dark bloom.

---

## Rovagug’s Hidden Influence
Though nameless in public worship, Rovagug’s touch lingers:  
- The **Spire’s resonance** occasionally distorts divine spells, leaving spiral-shaped burn patterns.  
- The [[Sanctified Guard]] forbids excavation beneath Respite, claiming “old containment wards” rest there.  
- Certain hymns of [[Sarenrae]] and [[Shelyn]] still bear altered verses once meant as prayers of sealing.

The cult of [[Serenya Vaelith]] — *The Chained Prophet* — is the most recent heretical rebirth of his faith.  
Her charities form the geometry of his prison key; her miracles, the first fractures in his chains.

---

### Forbidden Texts
- **The Annulus Codex:** A banned manuscript said to contain the original Covenant Oath binding Rovagug. Rumored fragments rest within the [[Monastery of the Perfect Quill]].  
- **The Spiral Canticles:** A series of hymns disguised as children’s lullabies; their geometry forms sigils of unbinding when sung in sequence.  
- **The Hollow Sun Sermons:** Destroyed by order of the [[Pontifex of Respite]]; written by a “Dawnmother” whose name was struck from record — almost certainly [[Serenya Vaelith]].  

---

## The Seal Beneath the Spire
At the Spire’s deepest foundation lies the **Annulus**, a divine lattice that hums with ceaseless light.  
According to hidden records kept by the Pharasmins, each god bound part of their essence into the seal — a thousand souls turned into a single hymn.  
Should the Annulus break, that song will invert, and the silence that follows will unmake the world.
