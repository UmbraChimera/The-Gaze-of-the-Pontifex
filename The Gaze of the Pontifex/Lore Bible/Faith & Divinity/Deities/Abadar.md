# 🪙 Abadar — The Master of the First Vault
**Titles:** The Gold-Fisted, The Judge of Cities  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Domains:** Cities, Wealth, Law, Civilization  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Light Crossbow  
**Symbol:** Golden key or cityscape enclosed within a coin  
**Sacred Animal:** Horse  

---

## Edicts
- Work diligently and honestly.  
- Bring law and order to lawless places.  
- Repay debts and uphold contracts.  
- Invest in civilization’s growth and infrastructure.  

## Anathema
- Cheat in trade or taxation.  
- Undermine legal systems for personal gain.  
- Destroy a building without rebuilding something greater.  

---

## Abadar’s Faith in Respite
Abadar’s temple dominates the **Crown of Faith** district, its façade lined with marble pillars and bronze scales of justice.  
The faithful serve as magistrates, notaries, architects, and bankers — the clerical machine that keeps the city running.  

In Respite’s politics, Abadar’s clergy are the **bureaucratic backbone**: strict, organized, and utterly convinced that civilization’s order is divine will.  
Their power rivals even the Pontifex’s, as every tithe, law, and contract ultimately passes through an Abadaran clerk’s hands.

> *“Law is the spine of mercy; without it, the city collapses under its own virtue.”*  

---

## The Golden Ledger
The temple’s sacred tome, *The Golden Ledger*, is a constantly updated document containing every major contract and decree of Respite — a bureaucratic relic said to rewrite itself when new laws pass the Conclave vote.
