# 🌠 Desna — The Song of the Spheres
**Titles:** The Great Dreamer, Lady of Dreams, The North Star  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
**Domains:** Dreams, Stars, Travelers, Luck  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Starknife  
**Symbol:** Butterfly with star-patterned wings  
**Sacred Animal:** Butterfly  

---

## Edicts
- Follow your dreams and your heart’s wanderlust.  
- Aid travelers, wanderers, and the lost.  
- Seek joy, love, and freedom wherever the world cages them.  

## Anathema
- Destroy or imprison the innocent.  
- Stifle freedom of movement or choice.  
- Harm a creature in its sleep or dreams.

---

## Desna’s Faith in Respite
Desna’s faithful in Respite are a blend of artists, explorers, and dream-seers who interpret the night sky as divine scripture.  
Her temple, **The Starlit Path**, crowns one of the city’s highest terraces, its open dome revealing the heavens.  

The **Starseers** act as navigators, prophets, and poets.  To the Reformers, they are the spiritual counterweight to Abadar’s bureaucrats — voices of freedom in a city of law.  
Her songs echo from balconies at night, carrying both hope and quiet rebellion.

> *“Even in shadow, the stars remember their light.”*
