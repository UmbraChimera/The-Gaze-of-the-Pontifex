# 🌊 Gozreh — The Wind and the Waves
**Titles:** The Storm Lord, The Everflowing, The Two-in-One  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Domains:** Nature, Storms, Sea, Sky  
**Divine Font:** Heal or Harm  
**Favored Weapon:** Trident  
**Symbol:** Swirling cloud and cresting wave intertwined  
**Sacred Animal:** Sea turtle  

---

## Edicts
- Live in balance with nature’s cycles.  
- Protect waterways, forests, and the natural order.  
- Respect the sea’s bounty and wrath equally.  
- Adapt and endure change as the world does.

## Anathema
- Pollute or exploit nature’s resources.  
- Waste food, water, or the gifts of the land.  
- Attempt to dominate the natural world through tyranny or greed.  

---

## Gozreh’s Faith in Respite
Gozreh’s clergy serve as **weatherwardens** — elemental priests who regulate rainfall, channel wind currents, and preserve the River Aurelion’s flow.  
They maintain the city’s aqueducts and coastal barriers with divine precision, their prayers woven into enchantments that temper storms.  

Within the Conclave, they act as mediators between Reformers and Traditionalists. They view politics as weather—tempestuous but temporary—and their aim is equilibrium.  

> *“The wind bows to no king.”*
