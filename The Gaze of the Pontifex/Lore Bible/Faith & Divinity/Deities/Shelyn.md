# 🎨 Shelyn — The Eternal Rose
**Titles:** The Eternal Rose, The Incorruptible Heart, The Lady of Art and Beauty  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Domains:** Art, Love, Beauty  
**Divine Font:** Heal  
**Favored Weapon:** Glaive  
**Symbol:** Songbird and a lyre intertwined by a single rose  
**Sacred Animal:** Swan  

---

## Edicts
- Create, share, and protect beauty in all forms.  
- Love freely and without prejudice.  
- Inspire others through kindness, artistry, and compassion.  
- Forgive those who harm in ignorance, but never excuse cruelty.  

## Anathema
- Destroy art or suppress expression.  
- Allow love to be twisted by fear or control.  
- Commit acts of deliberate ugliness, physical or spiritual.  

---

## Shelyn’s Faith in Respite
Shelyn’s faith is both the soul and façade of Respite — beauty as governance, art as law.  
Her cathedral, **The Prism Choir**, crowns the Crown of Faith district, where sunlight refracts through stained glass to paint divine color across marble streets.  

Her clergy are artists, musicians, and lawmakers who see legislation as a performance — one that must be graceful, fair, and eternally inspiring.  
Under her banner, the Pontifex rules as *Coronal*, blending faith and art into a single aesthetic ideal: **unity through beauty**.

> *“A beautiful law binds better than chains.”*
