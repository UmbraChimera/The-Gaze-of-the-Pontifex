# 🕳️ The Black Tribunal
The **Black Tribunal** is the shadow beneath Respite’s light — the Asmodean inquisition that ensures obedience through fear and control.  
Officially, it is a judicial arm of the [[Sanctified Guard]], sanctioned by the [[Conclave of Faiths]] to enforce divine law when faith alone fails.  
In truth, it answers only to [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] and the iron will of [[Asmodeus]].

> *“Mercy is chaos. Order must bleed.”*

---

## Overview
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
- **Faith:** [[Asmodeus]] — The Prince of Darkness  
- **Headquarters:** [[The Iron Bastion]] (within [[The Bastion Wall]])  
- **Leader:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] — Voice of Judgment, Enforcer of the Chain  
- **Primary Function:** Internal security, interrogation, heresy suppression, and divine espionage  
- **Motto:** *“Order through fear. Unity through silence.”*

---

## Role in Respite
The Tribunal acts as the unseen spine of Respite’s theocracy.  
Where the [[Sanctified Guard]] enforces divine law with honor, the Black Tribunal enforces it *absolutely*.  
Their inquisitors monitor every faith for deviation, rooting out heresy, corruption, or dissent — especially among other clergy.

Though publicly denounced as “necessary evil,” the Tribunal’s efficiency is unmatched.  
They alone possess the authority to interrogate priests, Pontifical aides, or even members of the Conclave under divine law.

> *“The gods built chains to bind Rovagug. We build chains to bind sin.”*

---

## Hierarchy
1. **Prelate of Chains:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] — supreme head of the Tribunal; reports directly to the [[Pontifex of Respite]] but takes orders from none.  
2. **Justiciars of Iron:** Senior inquisitors and executioners; command local tribunals across the districts.  
3. **Iron Adepts:** Spellbinding priests trained in pain-based divinations.  
4. **Scriptors:** Record keepers who transcribe confessions into enchanted scrolls stored in the [[Infernal Archive]]. 
5. **Acolytes of Order:** Low-ranking enforcers embedded within the [[Sanctified Guard]] and district courts.  

Every rank wears the same black-and-crimson tabard to obscure identity; only the Prelate’s chain sigil distinguishes him.

---

## Methods & Doctrine
The Black Tribunal enforces **The Nine Precepts of Obedience**, an Asmodean code of control:
1. Obedience is sanctity.  
2. Fear purifies faster than faith.  
3. Pain reveals truth.  
4. Mercy breeds rebellion.  
5. Silence is devotion.  
6. Law is divine.  
7. Chaos must be bound.  
8. Secrets are weapons.  
9. Unity demands sacrifice.  

Tribunal inquisitors operate under *Divine Writs of Authority*, allowing them to arrest, detain, or “purify” any citizen — even priests — without trial.

---

## Presence Across Respite
- **[[The Iron Bastion]]:** Primary fortress and interrogation hall.  
- **[[Crown Administrative Ward]]:** Maintains silent auditors within the [[Bureau of Divine Decrees]].  
- **[[Hammergate]]:** Monitors forge output to prevent “divine weapon heresy.”  
- **[[Halls of Relief]]:** Disguised agents infiltrate charities, searching for Serenya’s cult remnants.  
- **[[Under-Spire]]:** Operates a forbidden watchpost near the [[The Annulus]].  

The Tribunal’s eyes are everywhere, but their footsteps are never heard.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The Tribunal’s **Infernal Archive** contains rewritten confessions of historical crimes — including those of early Pontifexes.  
- [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] holds a contract binding the Pontifex himself — written in infernal script, witnessed by the Spire’s reflection.  
- A splinter faction within the Tribunal has begun worshipping *“The Silent Flame”* — believing Rovagug is Asmodeus’s reflection, not his enemy.  
- Several members of the [[Sanctified Guard]] have defected to serve as silent informants in exchange for absolution from unknown crimes.
