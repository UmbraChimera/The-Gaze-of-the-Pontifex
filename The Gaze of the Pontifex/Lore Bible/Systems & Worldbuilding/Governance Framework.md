# 🏛️ Respite Governance Framework
Every faction, guild, and civic group in **[[Respite]]** ultimately answers to a higher power.  
The city’s theocracy ensures that **faith defines authority**—no guild or order operates without divine oversight.  

> **Common Saying:** “All work is worship; all law is divine.”

---

## ⚙️ The Three Layers of Governance
1. **Faction Level:** Everyday leadership—guild masters, union heads, and company boards that run local affairs.  
2. **Initial Governing Body:** The first level of justice—internal tribunals, trade councils, or city patrols that arbitrate routine disputes.  
3. **Temple Escalation:** When cases surpass mortal reach, disputes ascend to the **temple** of the appropriate deity represented in the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
   - Escalations are both civic and spiritual trials.  
   - Verdicts rendered at this level are considered divine law.

The backbone of Respite’s economy, centered in [[Hammergate]].  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Hammergate Unionists** | Union councils | [[Torag]] | Dwarven-led labor alliance. Negotiates fair wages yet resists reckless automation; closely aligned with Torag’s Iron Sentinels. |
| **Glasswrights’ Guild** | Craft tribunal | [[Nethys]] | Crafts arcane glass and focusing lenses for temples and labs; production feeds the Spire’s resonance network. |
| **Masons’ Guild** | Masons’ court | [[Torag]] | Builds city fortifications; rumored to have rediscovered runic blueprints near the Spire’s base. |

> **GM Use:** Industry disputes = sabotage, missing plans, or heretical machines hidden in factory blueprints.

## 💰 Commerce & Finance
Trade is the city’s bloodstream, monitored through temple-sanctioned contracts.  

| Faction | Governing Body | Escalation | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Aurelion Guild Compact** | Merchant tribunal | [[Abadar]] | Manages markets and tariffs. Every deal is notarized; Abadaran clerks say “unwritten profit is sin.” |
| **Moneylenders’ Guild** | Lenders’ board | [[Abadar]] | Upholds fair-debt law and collateral codes. Ruthless but audited; contract disputes go straight to the Hall of Scales. |
| **Dockworkers’ Guild** | Harbor tribunal | [[Gozreh]] | Oversees River Aurelion traffic and quay safety; storms, tides, and quotas fall under the Warden of the Current. |

> **GM Use:** Corruption here can spark citywide crises or moral dilemmas about fairness vs. greed.

## Notes for Play (GM)
- The divine bureaucracy gives structure to every story: a theft, trial, or miracle always escalates to faith.  
- Use guild disputes as inciting incidents—each reflects its temple’s ethos.  
- The multi-layered governance means no decision is purely civic; **every law is divine politics.**
