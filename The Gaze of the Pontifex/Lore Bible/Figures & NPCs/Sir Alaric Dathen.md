## Sir Alaric Dathen
**Affiliation:** [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith|Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]  
**Race:** Human  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good  
**Title:** Captain of the People’s Guard (Ceremonial)  
**Bloc:** Reformer (sympathizer)  

### Public Reputation
Known as “the Smiling Sword.” The people adore his optimism and humility; soldiers admire his loyalty.

### Personality
Cheerful, trusting, genuinely good-hearted. Sees the best in everyone.

### Mannerisms & Voice
Earnest tone, straightforward speech. Touches his holy symbol when nervous.

### Goals
- Protect Serenya and her reforms.  
- Bring peace to the streets through compassion, not fear.  

### Relationships
- Idolizes [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith|Serenya]].  
- Respected by the [[Sanctified Guard|Sanctified Guard]].  
- Cordially tolerated by [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion|Thalanil]].  

### Secrets
- Unaware his faith in Serenya will lead to his death.
