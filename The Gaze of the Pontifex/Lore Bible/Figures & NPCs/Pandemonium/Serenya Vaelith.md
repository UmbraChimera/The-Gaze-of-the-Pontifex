## Serenya Vaelith — The Chained Prophet
**Deity:** [[Rovagug]]  
**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
**Title:** The Chained Prophet, Voice of the Spiral  
**Bloc:** None (hidden)  

---

### Public Reputation
None.  
Serenya’s secret identity as the Chained Prophet is known only to a few of her most fanatical followers.  
To the world, she remains the radiant *Dawnmother* of [[Sarenrae]] — beloved, merciful, and incorruptible.  
But beneath that halo burns something far older, a divine whisper promising *“redemption through ruin.”*

If revealed, her betrayal would not merely wound Respite’s faith — it would annihilate it.

---

### Personality
Serene, gentle, and chillingly rational.  
She believes that mercy is meaningless without suffering and that true peace can only be achieved through the dissolution of all creation.  
In her eyes, destruction is not cruelty — it is **grace**.

---

### Mannerisms & Voice
Soft-spoken and melodic, her tone is a balm that conceals the venom beneath.  
She speaks of apocalypse the way a healer speaks of medicine: calm, inevitable, and necessary.  
Her eyes shimmer like dawnlight refracted through water — radiant, but hollow.

---

### Goals
- Complete the **Rite of the Spiral**, an apocalyptic ritual woven into her charitable works.  
- **Sacrifice [[Sir Alaric Dathen]]** to shatter the divine lock beneath the [[The Spire]] — a living keystone to unleash [[Rovagug]]’s essence.  
- Merge the divine and mortal realms, “so that gods may die as men do, and men may finally rest.”  

---

### Relationships
- **Former Ally:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]). Once saw him as the perfect counterpart — creation and destruction in harmony — until she deemed him too weak to complete the cycle.  
- **Manipulator:** [[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong]] ([[Erastil]]). Uses her halfling networks to move goods and agents unseen.  
- **Beloved Pawn:** [[Sir Alaric Dathen]] — the sacrifice she both adores and condemns.  
- **Silent Confidant:** A nameless figure in her dreams, whom she believes to be [[Rovagug]] himself whispering through the Spire’s hum.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Serenya’s divine magic is no longer purely Sarenrite. It is **tainted by the Chained God’s resonance** within the Spire. Her healing spells always leave faint spiral scars — marks of the coming unbinding.  
- Her charities, orphanages, and pilgrim shelters form a **geometric lattice** across Respite — a living rune array that channels prayer into a single, catastrophic point beneath the city.  
- Her **soul is bound** to the Spire itself; even if slain, her essence will remain dormant within the structure, waiting for a new vessel to rekindle the Spiral.  
- The *Rite of the Spiral* requires the **blood of the faithful** — the mass devotion of Respite’s people — willingly given in prayer.  
- She believes that when the Spiral completes, the Spire will bloom into a *“flower of ending,”* its light consuming gods and mortals alike.  

---

### Symbolism
Her private sigil replaces Sarenrae’s rising sun with a **spiral sun collapsing inward**, ringed with faint runic chains.  
Her followers wear pendants shaped like stylized sunbursts of molten glass, each etched with a spiral visible only under moonlight.
