## Prelate Korven Dreadmark — The Iron Law
**Race:** Human (Chelaxian)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Prelate of the Black Tribunal  
**Role:** Representative of [[Asmodeus]], Lord of Tyranny and Law  

---

### Public Reputation
Korven Dreadmark is the name every child learns to fear and every bureaucrat prays to avoid.  
To the faithful, he is the perfect instrument of discipline. To the common folk, he is the living embodiment of the chain.  

He appears in immaculate crimson robes, his every motion deliberate — a man carved from law itself.

---

### Personality & Voice
Polite, eloquent, and chillingly patient.  
Speaks with the cadence of legal scripture, turning even pleasantries into veiled decrees.  
Believes pain and obedience are forms of enlightenment.

---

### Goals
- Maintain absolute order in Respite, even if through terror.  
- Expand the reach of the **Black Tribunal** into the [[Sanctified Guard]].  
- Bind the Conclave through contracts so tight that rebellion becomes heresy.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) — shares his respect for law, though not his mercy.  
- **Rival:** [[High Justiciar Arria Varus]] ([[Iomedae]]); sees her compassion as weakness.  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]) for maintaining hierarchy under grace.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Possesses a **contract written in infernal script** binding the Pontifex himself — the exact terms remain unknown, but it ensures the Tribunal’s “eternal necessity.”  
- Keeps an infernal familiar disguised as a raven, known as *Veris*, which records every conversation within the Hall of Concord.  
- His true ambition is not power, but permanence: to reshape Respite’s divine law so it outlives gods and mortals alike.
