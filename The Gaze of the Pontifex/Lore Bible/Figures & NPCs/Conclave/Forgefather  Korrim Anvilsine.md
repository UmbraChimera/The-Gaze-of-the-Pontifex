## Forgefather Korrim Anvilsine — Hammergate’s Heart
**Race:** Dwarf (Mendevian)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Forgefather of Hammergate  
**Role:** Representative of [[Torag]], God of Forge, Protection, and Strategy  

---

### Public Reputation
Korrim is beloved among the city’s laborers and smiths.  
Known as the “Father of Fire,” he’s a symbol of honest work and craftsmanship.  
His booming laugh and soot-streaked beard are as much landmarks as the forges themselves.

---

### Personality & Voice
Gruff, direct, and deeply paternal.  
Speaks in metaphor—fire, metal, and pressure as lessons of life.  
Known to end political debates with a simple: “That won’t hold under the hammer.”

---

### Goals
- Keep Hammergate’s forges operational despite curfew restrictions.  
- Establish fair wages and working conditions for the laboring class.  
- Reinforce bridges and buildings around the [[The Spire]] to withstand arcane resonance.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) and [[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong]] ([[Erastil]]).  
- **Rival:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]); views his cruelty as corrosion of divine order.  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]); art as another form of craftsmanship.

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Secretly funds an underground worker’s fellowship known as *The Union of Ember*, a semi-legal organization that supports Serenya’s reforms through trade disruptions.  
- The anvil at his personal forge hums faintly with divine resonance when the [[The Spire]] shifts — he believes it’s reacting to buried dwarven runes beneath the city.  
- Keeps a weapon forged from the Spire’s outer fragments—“The Silent Hammer”—hidden within his temple vault.
