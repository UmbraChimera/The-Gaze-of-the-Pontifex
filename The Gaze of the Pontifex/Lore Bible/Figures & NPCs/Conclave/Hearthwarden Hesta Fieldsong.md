## Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong — The Hearth’s Voice
**Race:** Halfling  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** Hearthwarden of the Harvest  
**Role:** Representative of [[Erastil]], God of Family and Farming  

---

### Public Reputation
Beloved by commoners and pilgrims alike, Hesta is a grandmotherly figure to all.  
Her laughter fills markets, her bread fills bellies, and her words often silence the Conclave’s fiercest debates.

### Personality & Voice
Warm, patient, and unshakably kind.  
Speaks in parables and household metaphors; her tone softens even the most arrogant priest.  

### Goals
- Protect the poor from taxation and abuse.  
- Secure food distribution across Respite during curfews.  
- Encourage trade between farmers and Hammergate workers.  

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]] ([[Torag]]).  
- **Rival:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]), whom she calls “the iron worm.”  
- **Respects:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]) for her compassion.  

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Maintains a secret Desnan shrine beneath her home to honor freedom and dreams.  
- Her “village choir” doubles as an underground courier network.  
- Has prophetic dreams of the [[The Spire]]’s light dimming during winter’s end.
