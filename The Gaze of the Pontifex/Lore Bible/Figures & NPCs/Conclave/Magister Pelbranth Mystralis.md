## Magister Pelbranth Mystralis — The Eye Between Worlds
**Race:** Garundi (Human)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Magister of the Weave  
**Role:** Representative of [[Nethys]], God of Magic and Balance  

---

### Public Reputation
Pelbranth is a paradox — aloof yet omnipresent, eccentric yet precise.  
To the public, he is “the city’s eye,” the mage who sees all that happens near the [[The Spire]].  
He is both respected and feared for his ability to unravel enchantments with a gesture and to silence storms with a single word.

---

### Personality & Voice
Speaks in alternating tones — one serene, one fervent, as if two minds share his body.  
Sometimes forgets which he is addressing: the Conclave or the gods.  
Smiles rarely, except when witnessing new magic or chaos.

---

### Goals
- Maintain equilibrium between divine miracles and mortal spellcraft.  
- Prevent reckless use of magic near the [[The Spire]].  
- Study the harmonic resonance between divine prayers and arcane wards.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Ascetic Dhenar Rajesh]] ([[Irori]]) — they share mutual respect for discipline.  
- **Rival:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]); views her miracles as unregulated power.  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]) for her mastery of divine artistry.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Suffers from “Dual Sight” — a magical condition where both of Nethys’s aspects speak through him simultaneously.  
- Keeps forbidden tomes detailing the Spire’s anti-magic fields; believes the structure was built as a divine containment vessel.  
- Privately sympathizes with Reformers, but fears that destabilizing the Conclave could collapse the Weave itself.
