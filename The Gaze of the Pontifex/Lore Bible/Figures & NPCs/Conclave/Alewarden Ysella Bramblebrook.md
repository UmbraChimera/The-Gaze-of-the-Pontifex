## High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook — The Smile of the People
**Race:** Halfling  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** Alewarden of the Hero’s Rest  
**Role:** Representative of [[Cayden Cailean]], God of Freedom and Bravery  

---

### Public Reputation
Ysella Bramblebrook is the soul of Respite’s commoners — beloved by laborers, adored by children, and tolerated (barely) by nobles.  
Her temple’s tavern is where city laws are debated long before they reach the Conclave.  

She is seen as both saint and scoundrel — a woman who toasts with saints, laughs with sinners, and somehow earns both their blessings.

---

### Personality & Voice
Bold, bawdy, and compassionate.  
Speaks in colloquial metaphors, often while drinking mid-sentence.  
Believes that “truth sounds best after a pint or two.”  

---

### Goals
- Protect the rights of workers and tavern guilds.  
- Challenge laws that infringe on joy or freedom.  
- Forge alliances between Reformers and sympathetic Traditionalists.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong]] ([[Erastil]]) and [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]).  
- **Rival:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]) — she calls him “The Hangover of Respite.”  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]), as art and joy share sacred roots.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Keeps coded “poetry” in her tavern ledgers — messages between Reformist spies and union leaders.  
- Once smuggled forbidden wine from the Spire’s lower levels — a divine brew said to make mortals dream of godhood.  
- Has visions of Cayden laughing in her dreams, warning her to “keep faith when the cup turns black.”
