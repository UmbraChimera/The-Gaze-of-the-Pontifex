## Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith — The Light of the People
**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** Dawnmother of the Cathedral of the Dawn  
**Role:** Representative of [[Sarenrae]], Goddess of Healing and Redemption  

---

### Public Reputation
Serenya is adored by the commonfolk and worshipped by the desperate.  
Her compassion feels limitless; her charisma, divine.  
In the streets, murals depict her standing in sunlight pouring from [[The Spire]] — *The Living Dawn.*  

To her followers, she is mercy incarnate.  
To her rivals, she is a populist heretic with dangerous influence.  

---

### Personality & Voice
Warm, radiant, and articulate.  
Her words are measured for the masses, yet her eyes hint at something calculating beneath the glow.  
She carries herself with saintly humility but radiates command like the sun itself.  

---

### Goals
- Reform Respite’s laws to favor compassion over punishment.  
- Break the hold of the Traditionalists and elevate the poor to civic representation.  
- “Renew the Covenant” — a public project of unity that secretly masks a deeper, apocalyptic ritual.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Starseer Mirathiel Silver-Eye]] ([[Desna]]) and [[Alewarden Ysella Bramblebrook]] ([[Cayden Cailean]]).  
- **Rivals:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) and [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]).  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]), calling him “The Light’s Reflection.”  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Serenya’s miracles are genuine, but their power is drawn not just from Sarenrae — the [[The Spire]] amplifies her magic in ways she does not fully control.  
- Her “Covenant Renewal” is a massive ritual intended to “purge corruption” — in truth, a working that will weaken the divine seal beneath the Spire.  
- She has convinced herself that this act will “redeem the gods themselves” by freeing what she believes to be divine potential — actually the influence of [[Rovagug]].
