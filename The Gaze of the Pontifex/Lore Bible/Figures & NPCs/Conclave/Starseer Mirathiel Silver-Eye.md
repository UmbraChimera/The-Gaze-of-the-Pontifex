## Starseer Mirathiel Silver-Eye — The Dreaming Voice
**Race:** Elf (Varisian heritage)  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** Starseer of the Starlit Path  
**Role:** Representative of [[Desna]], Goddess of Dreams and Freedom  

---

### Public Reputation
Mirathiel is adored by pilgrims and artists and regarded with wary fascination by scholars.  
She is said to dream of the future in fragments of starlight.  
Her speeches feel like poetry; her prophecies, like lullabies that terrify and comfort in equal measure.

---

### Personality & Voice
Soft, melodic, and detached from urgency.  
Her gaze seems always fixed beyond the Spire, seeing horizons others cannot.  
Laughs easily but mourns every loss as though it were written in the stars.

---

### Goals
- Protect the rights of the poor to travel and trade without curfew.  
- Interpret the dreams of the populace as divine messages from Desna.  
- Convince the Conclave to re-open the Pilgrim Roads closed by the Pontifex’s edict.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]) — together form the Reformer heart of the Conclave.  
- **Rival:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) — whom she calls “a man of ledgers, not horizons.”  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]); views his art as worship that touches Desna’s stars.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Her right eye — the “Silver Eye” — is not natural; it’s a celestial shard that fell from the night sky during her youth.  It allows her to see the dream-realm overlapping Respite.  
- She knows the Spire’s light pattern mirrors a constellation that has not existed for millennia — and that its return would mark a divine convergence.  
- Keeps a map of dream-routes hidden in her temple — paths through the Ethereal that touch all sixteen temples at once.
