## High Justiciar Arria Varus — The Blade of Virtue
**Race:** Human (Taldan)  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** High Justiciar of the Blades of Valor  
**Role:** Representative of [[Iomedae]], Goddess of Honor and Valor  

---

### Public Reputation
Revered as a paragon of justice and feared as its executioner.  
Citizens whisper her name when they speak of fair trials and swift punishments.  
To the Guard, she is *The Silver Sword* — unyielding yet incorruptible.

---

### Personality & Voice
Stern but compassionate.  
Her speech is precise and ritualized, each word weighted as though a vow.  
Rarely laughs, but often smiles when others choose mercy.

---

### Goals
- Cleanse the [[Sanctified Guard]] of corruption.  
- Uphold justice even when the Pontifex’s edicts conflict with morality.  
- Train a new generation of paladins untainted by politics.

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Ally:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]); law as their shared language.  
- **Rival:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]); sees his “order through fear” as blasphemy.  
- **Respects:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]) for her compassion, though deems it naïve.

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Suffers visions of a burning Spire where justice is weighed and found wanting. She believes these to be Iomedae’s warnings.  
- Her mithral prosthetic arm was crafted by [[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]] ([[Torag]]), and houses a hidden blade that glows when detecting lies.
