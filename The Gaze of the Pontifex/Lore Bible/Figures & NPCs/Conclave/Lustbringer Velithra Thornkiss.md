## Lustbringer Velithra Thornkiss — The Sting Beneath the Smile
**Race:** Elf  
**Gender:** Female  
**Title:** Lustbringer of the House of Whispered Lace  
**Role:** Representative of [[Calistria]], Goddess of Lust, Revenge, and Trickery  

---

### Public Reputation
Velithra is a paradox of Respite — scandalous and sacred.  
She is the whisper in noble courts and the muse behind half the city’s poetry.  
To the devout, she is Calistria’s chosen avatar of allure and vengeance; to the prudish, she is the slow poison of reform.  

Her beauty is matched only by her intellect — every word a weapon wrapped in silk.  

---

### Personality & Voice
Velithra’s speech flows like honey over razors — slow, deliberate, and enticing.  
She moves as though performing for unseen gods, never still, never predictable.  
Her laughter makes even the Pontifex blush; her silences make him sweat.  

---

### Goals
- Strengthen the Reformer bloc through influence and blackmail.  
- Replace the Pontifex’s spies with her own network of “entertainers.”  
- Redeem the art of pleasure as divine, not shameful.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]) — her patron of compassion.  
- **Rival:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) — “the man who sold joy for a coin.”  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]); their rivalry in artistry is infamous and flirtatious.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Holds compromising letters between several Conclave members, including one that could destroy Abadar’s reputation if revealed.  
- Her divine mark (a golden wasp tattoo) moves along her skin at will — a literal sting of Calistria.  
- Secretly arranges “peace duels” between rival priests — each a public spectacle masking assassinations ordered by her temple.
