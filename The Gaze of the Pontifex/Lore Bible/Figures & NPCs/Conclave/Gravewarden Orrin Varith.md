## Gravewarden Orrin Varith — The Keeper of the Veil
**Race:** Human (Varisian)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Gravewarden of Passing  
**Role:** Representative of [[Pharasma]], Lady of Graves  

---

### Public Reputation
Orrin is quiet, unflappable, and unnervingly calm.  
Citizens speak of him in whispers, calling him “The Walking Epitaph.”  
He is courteous to all, but his presence makes even the bold lower their voices.

---

### Personality & Voice
Soft, deliberate, and fatalistic.  
He ends every conversation with, “May your path spiral true.”  
Shows kindness to the grieving, impatience to the ambitious.

---

### Goals
- Maintain the sanctity of Respite’s necropolis.  
- Eradicate necromantic practices among nobles seeking immortality.  
- Expand the Scrolls of Silence to include not only deaths but divine oaths.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Ally:** [[Ascetic Dhenar Rajesh]] ([[Irori]]) — appreciates his calm and order.  
- **Rival:** [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] ([[Asmodeus]]) — calls him a “soul-thief in velvet.”  
- **Respects:** [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]) for her mercy, though believes she cannot comprehend true endings.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Discovered evidence that the [[The Spire]] is a divine tomb built over the remains of an ancient celestial.  
- Holds a list of nobles who illegally prolong life through alchemical means; uses it to manipulate political balance.  
- Keeps a black crystal — a fragment from beneath the Spire — that whispers to him in the voices of the newly dead.
