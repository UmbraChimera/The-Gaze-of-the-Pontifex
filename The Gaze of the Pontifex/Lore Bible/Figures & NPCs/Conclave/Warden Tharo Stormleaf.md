## Warden Tharo Stormleaf — The Voice of the Current
**Race:** Shoanti (Half-Orc)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Warden of the River and Sky  
**Role:** Representative of [[Gozreh]], Deity of Nature, Storms, and the Sea  

---

### Public Reputation
Called “The River’s Temper,” Tharo is revered as both guardian and judge of the Aurelion.  
His moods shift like the weather: tranquil one day, storming the next.  
Sailors whisper that when Tharo is angry, lightning strikes closer to the docks.

---

### Personality & Voice
Speaks in a rhythmic cadence — low and resonant, like waves on stone.  
His laugh is deep and sudden, rolling like thunder; his temper is just as swift.  
Believes sincerity is sacred, and hypocrisy is poison.

---

### Goals
- Keep the River Aurelion clean and flowing.  
- Ensure Hammergate’s forges do not choke the city’s air.  
- Maintain neutrality within the Conclave; balance must prevail.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong]] ([[Erastil]]) and [[Serenya Vaelith]] ([[Sarenrae]]).  
- **Rival:** [[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]] ([[Torag]]); their feud over forge pollution is legendary.  
- **Respects:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] ([[Shelyn]]); calls art “a mirror for nature.”  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Keeps an imprisoned water elemental beneath the docks — a creature known as *The Tidebound* — which he believes carries messages from Gozreh.  
- Has seen strange ripples of light moving *upstream* toward [[The Spire]]; he fears the city’s foundations are disrupting natural flow.  
- Accidentally caused a noble’s ship to sink years ago during an “uncontrolled storm,” but the records were erased by Abadaran clerks who owe him favors.
