## Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear — The Crimson Vanguard
**Race:** Dwarf (Ulfen heritage)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Battlelord of the Crimson Host  
**Role:** Representative of [[Gorum]], Lord in Iron  

---

### Public Reputation
Dorgar is a living legend of Respite’s battlefields — a warrior-priest whose laughter carries over the clang of steel.  
He is adored by the city’s soldiers and feared by its politicians.  
Wherever conflict brews, Dorgar appears, hammer in hand and grin beneath his beard.

---

### Personality & Voice
Loud, blunt, and prone to dueling in the middle of debates.  
His booming voice commands attention; he punctuates his words by striking his armor with a gauntlet.  
Despite his ferocity, he bears no malice — every fight is sacred play to him.

---

### Goals
- Keep the [[Sanctified Guard]] prepared for true war, not ceremony.  
- Ensure military funding remains independent of Abadar’s economic influence.  
- Prove that divine will is measured in courage, not laws.  

---

### Relationships & Politics
- **Allies:** [[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]] ([[Torag]]) — brotherhood of the forge and the battlefield.  
- **Rival:** [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] ([[Abadar]]) — calls him “coin in armor.”  
- **Respects:** [[High Justiciar Arria Varus]] ([[Iomedae]]) for her discipline, even if he mocks her restraint.  

---

### Secrets (GM Only)
- Once led a secret mission to clear undead from the Spire’s foundation — but claims something *fought back from within*.  
- His “sacred armor” is bound with faint runes that hum when  [[The Spire]] pulses — he believes it calls warriors to destiny.
