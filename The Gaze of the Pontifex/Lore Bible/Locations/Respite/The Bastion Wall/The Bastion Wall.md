# ⚔️ The Bastion Wall
The **Bastion Wall** is both fortress and faith — a 400-foot-high barrier encircling [[Respite]].  
It is the city’s first and last defense, a monument to vigilance carved in white stone streaked with divine metal.  
The wall gleams day and night, reflecting the [[The Spire]]’s glow like a halo.

No army has ever breached it. Many have tried.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~40,000 (soldiers, engineers, and their families)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Iomedae]], [[Torag]], [[Gorum]]  
- **Primary Function:** Defense, training, and fortification  
- **Atmosphere:** Regimented, disciplined, humming with readiness.  

---

## Architecture
The Bastion Wall is more than a wall — it is a city upon a wall.  
Barracks, armories, temples, and even markets line its ramparts.  
Massive statues of divine guardians mark each quadrant, their eyes glowing faintly with holy light.  
The outer face bears 16 bas-reliefs — one for each deity of the Conclave — locked in an eternal ring of protection.

---

## Leadership
- **[[High Justiciar Arria Varus]]** ([[Iomedae]]) — Oversees legal authority of the Guard and trials of war.  
- **[[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]]** ([[Torag]]) — Maintains structural integrity and divine wards of the wall.  
- **[[Battlelord Dorgar Ironspear]]** ([[Gorum]]) — Commands the outer defenses and oversees martial readiness.  

---

## Faith Presence
- [[The Sanctum Martial]] — Command bastion of the [[Sanctified Guard]].  
- [[The Stormwall]] — Gozreh’s wind-temple controlling weather wards around the city.  
- [[The Iron Bastion]] — The Black Tribunal’s hidden headquarters and interrogation halls.

---

## Economy
Military industries dominate.  
Smiths, fletchers, and quartermasters feed a constant demand for arms and armor.  
The **Wall Markets** sell goods to soldiers and mercenaries, ranging from rations to relics of protection.

---

## Crime & Corruption
Corruption wears armor here.  
Smuggling relics and falsifying conscription orders are the main crimes.  
The [[Black Tribunal]] operates a secret prison inside the wall — *The Chain Cells* — for “divine dissidents.”

> *“The wall keeps the world out — and sometimes, truth with it.”*

---

## Rumors

## GM Hooks
- The PCs are recruited to investigate sabotage in the wall’s forges — the molten metal “screamed” before hardening.  
- A defector claims that the Chain Cells hold something older than Respite itself.  
- A section of the wall “melts” one night — and reforms with a new, unknown symbol.
