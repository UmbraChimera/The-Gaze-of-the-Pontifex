# 🕳️ The Iron Bastion
The **Iron Bastion** is the secretive fortress of [[Asmodeus]]’s [[Black Tribunal]], carved into the Bastion Wall’s northern quadrant.  
It is both court and prison — a temple where fear is law and confession is salvation.

---

## Features
- **The Chamber of Chains:** Interrogation hall; every chain hums when a lie is spoken.  
- **The Infernal Archive:** Library of confessions magically extracted from the guilty.  
- **The Hall of Iron Oaths:** Hidden courtroom where rulings are made in total darkness.
