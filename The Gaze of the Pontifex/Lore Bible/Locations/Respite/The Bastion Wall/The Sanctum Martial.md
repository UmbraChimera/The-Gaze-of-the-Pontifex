# 🛡️ The Sanctum Martial
Headquarters of the [[Sanctified Guard]], the **Sanctum Martial** is the strategic heart of Respite’s defense.  
From here, sixteen divine orders coordinate as one army under the banner of the [[Pontifex of Respite]].

---

## Features
- **The Map of Flames:** Enchanted wall-sized map showing the entire city, updating magically when divine wards falter.  
- **The Hall of Shields:** Armory of the first Sanctified Guard — each shield still bears divine blessings.  
- **Command Sanctum:** War council chamber lit by sixteen braziers, each representing a deity.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The seventeenth brazier in the Command Sanctum is hidden; its chain pattern matches that of [[Rovagug]].  
- The Map of Flames flickers every time [[The Spire]] hums, briefly showing tunnels beneath the city.  
- [[Prelate Korven Dreadmark]] maintains spies here — “for efficiency.”
