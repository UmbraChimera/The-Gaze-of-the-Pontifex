# 🌩️ The Stormwall
The **Stormwall** is [[Gozreh]]’s temple-fortress built directly into the Bastion’s outer edge.  
Its priests command the weather itself to protect the city — summoning winds to repel sieges and rain to bless the fields beyond.

---

## Features
- **The Tempest Engine:** Massive crystal array controlling storm patterns over Respite.  
- **The Rainspire:** A vertical shaft through which lightning flows harmlessly into divine batteries.  
- **The Gale Chamber:** Open-roofed sanctum where Gozreh’s clergy commune with the sky.
