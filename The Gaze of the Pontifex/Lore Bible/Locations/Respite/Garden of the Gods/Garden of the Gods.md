# 🌿 Garden of the Gods
The **Garden of the Gods** is a vast sacred park at the city’s heart — neutral ground for all sixteen faiths.  
Lush greenery, mirrored pools, and divine sculptures fill this serene expanse, where mortals may pray beneath every god’s gaze without fear or politics.

Here, Respite breathes.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~20,000 (pilgrims, gardeners, acolytes)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Gozreh]], [[Pharasma]]  
- **Supporting Faiths:** All others maintain shrines.  
- **Primary Function:** Worship, rest, funerary rites, and neutral diplomacy.  
- **Atmosphere:** Peaceful, reverent, deceptively quiet.

---

## Architecture
The garden forms a perfect ring around the inner Spire avenues.  
Each faith’s shrine is placed according to ancient celestial geometry.  
The district shifts subtly with the seasons — spring fountains bloom for Shelyn, while autumn mists herald Gozreh’s storms.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Warden Tharo Stormleaf]]** ([[Gozreh]]) — Oversees nature’s harmony and elemental balance.  
- **[[Gravewarden Orrin Varith]]** ([[Pharasma]]) — Maintains funerary rites and interment laws.  
- **Keeper Alyra Nhalin** — Neutral caretaker who records the prayers spoken in the central grove.

---

## Economy
Few taxes, endless donations.  
The Garden’s holy relic trade is the most subtle form of economy in Respite — flowers from Shelyn’s vines, droplets of consecrated water, even pebbles “kissed by light.”

---

## Faith Presence
- [[Whispering Shrines]] — Silent sanctuary where all gods share space.  
- [[Fountain of Petals]] — Shelyn’s sacred spring of beauty and renewal.  
- [[Silent Grove]] — Pharasmin funerary garden where souls are laid to rest.

---

## Crime & Corruption
None visible — yet.  
Rumors claim [[Lustbringer Velithra Thornkiss]] uses the secluded paths for diplomatic liaisons, and the [[Black Tribunal]] buries evidence beneath consecrated soil.

> *“All gods walk here. Some linger longer than they should.”*
