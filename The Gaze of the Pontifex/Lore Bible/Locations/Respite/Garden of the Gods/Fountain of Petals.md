# 🌸 The Fountain of Petals
*Shelyn’s sacred spring of beauty and renewal.*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Garden of the Gods]]  
**Custodian:** Artists and priests of [[Shelyn]]  
**Affiliation:** Central Shelynite sanctuary under the [[Pontifex of Respite]]  
**Purpose:** Healing and inspiration through art and beauty  

The **Fountain of Petals** is the spiritual heart of the Garden — a marble basin surrounded by flowering vines and colored glass. Its waters shimmer with faint chromatic light, each hue reflecting a facet of divine grace.  The Pontifex holds several public ceremonies here each year, proclaiming it *“Shelyn’s tear made water.”*

---

## Description
The fountain flows from a sculpture of intertwined figures, angelic and mortal, holding aloft a crystal lyre.  Blossoms fall constantly from nearby arches, carried by unseen wind.  Artists often sketch and sing here, as though inspiration itself lingers in the mist.

A quiet hum resonates in the stone — a perpetual hymn audible only to the faithful.

---

## Access & Restrictions
Pilgrims may approach freely but must leave weapons and holy symbols at the gate.  The [[Sanctified Guard]] maintains a respectful distance, honoring the space as the Pontifex’s favored site of reflection.  
Drawing water without blessing is forbidden, though petals from the basin may be taken as souvenirs of devotion.
