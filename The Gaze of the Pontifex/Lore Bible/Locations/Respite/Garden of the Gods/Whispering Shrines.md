# 🕯️ The Whispering Shrines
*Silent sanctuary where all gods share space.*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Garden of the Gods]]  
**Custodian:** Mendicant Order of the Sixteen  
**Affiliation:** Neutral ground overseen by the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Shared temple space for all divine patrons  

The **Whispering Shrines** sit at the Garden’s center — sixteen open alcoves surrounding a circular courtyard of white marble. No icons, idols, or banners mark the shrines; each is distinguished only by scent, sound, or texture. It is the only site in Respite where all gods are honored together, in silence.

---

## Description
Columns of alabaster rise like reeds around the shrines, catching the wind in hollow tones. The air smells faintly of oil and rain. Visitors pray without voice, tracing sigils into dust or water — gestures said to reach any deity who listens.

In the courtyard’s center stands a stone disk engraved with the symbol of the [[The Annulus]], overlaid with sixteen interlocking spirals.

---

## Access & Restrictions
The Shrines remain open day and night.  
By decree of the [[Pontifex of Respite]], no clergy may preach or collect tithe within its bounds. The [[Sanctified Guard]] patrol quietly but do not interfere.  
It is considered a grave offense to speak above a whisper within the inner ring.
