# 🏛️ House of Whispered Lace
*Temple of Calistria — Goddess of Lust, Revenge, and Trickery*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Garden of the Gods]]  
**Custodian:** [[Lustbringer Velithra Thornkiss]]  
**Affiliation:** Church of Calistria, recognized member of the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Sanctuary of desire, art, and secrets  

The House of Whispered Lace stands among Respite’s most contentious holy sites — a temple-pleasurehouse where devotion and indulgence entwine.  Its clergy preach that **truth is found through passion and vengeance**, and that every emotion is divine when sincerely expressed.

---

## Description
A crescent of gilded balconies and silken awnings wraps the inner gardens.  Curtains of lace shimmer with illusion magic that alters hue and texture with each visitor’s mood.  Within:  

- **The Veiled Stage:** masked performances recount Calistria’s acts of betrayal and ecstasy.  
- **The Mirror Grotto:** mirrored chambers where penitents confront their hidden desires.  
- **The Needle Garden:** rooftop of thorned golden roses; their scent is said to soothe jealousy.  

Music drifts continuously, blending harp and whispered laughter; incense carries a note of honey and venom.

---

## Access & Restrictions
Entry is open to any who tithe in coin or confession.  The Sanctified Guard maintain distance by Pontifical decree, though the [[Black Tribunal]] keeps informants among the temple’s attendants.  Private confessionals and the upper salons require a **token of favor** granted by Velithra herself.

Visitors are warned: all words spoken inside are magically recorded within silken wards — “to preserve the memory of honest passion.”

---

## Encounters & Secrets
- **Velvet Choir Courtiers** (Expert 5 / CN): courtesan-spies who trade information for experiences.  
- **Honeyblade Acolytes** (Rogue 4 / CN): oath-bound avengers serving broken lovers.  
- **Hidden Chamber beneath the Mirror Grotto:** sealed archive of whispered vows; accessible only through reflection magic.  
- **The Perfumer’s Cellar:** where incense ingredients double as rare poisons.

No evidence of heresy exists — only an uncomfortably efficient information network.

---

## Adventure Hooks
- **Masks of the Pontifex:** A stolen ceremonial mask reveals the Pontifex’s private visits here; the PCs must recover it before the Tribunal does.  
- **Perfume of Vengeance:** A courtesan requests protection after crafting a scent said to compel confessions.  
- **The Curtain Falls:** During a diplomatic masquerade, a performer collapses mid-aria — poisoned, or silenced for what she knew?

---

## Notes
The temple’s existence remains a public paradox — **condemned by law, protected by art**.  Many believe it survives only because the Pontifex himself values Calistria’s lessons in truth through beauty.
