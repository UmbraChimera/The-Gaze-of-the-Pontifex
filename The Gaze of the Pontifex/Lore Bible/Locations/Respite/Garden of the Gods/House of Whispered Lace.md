# 🏛️ House of Whispered Lace
*Temple of Calistria — Goddess of Lust, Revenge, and Trickery*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Garden of the Gods]]  
**Custodian:** [[Lustbringer Velithra Thornkiss]]  
**Affiliation:** Church of Calistria, recognized member of the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Sanctuary of desire, art, and secrets  

The House of Whispered Lace stands among Respite’s most contentious holy sites — a temple-pleasurehouse where devotion and indulgence entwine.  Its clergy preach that **truth is found through passion and vengeance**, and that every emotion is divine when sincerely expressed.

---

## Description
A crescent of gilded balconies and silken awnings wraps the inner gardens.  Curtains of lace shimmer with illusion magic that alters hue and texture with each visitor’s mood.  Within:  

- **The Veiled Stage:** masked performances recount Calistria’s acts of betrayal and ecstasy.  
- **The Mirror Grotto:** mirrored chambers where penitents confront their hidden desires.  
- **The Needle Garden:** rooftop of thorned golden roses; their scent is said to soothe jealousy.  

Music drifts continuously, blending harp and whispered laughter; incense carries a note of honey and venom.
