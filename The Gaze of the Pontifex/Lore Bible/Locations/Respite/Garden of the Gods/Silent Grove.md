# 🌲 The Silent Grove
*Pharasmin funerary garden where souls are laid to rest.*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Garden of the Gods]]  
**Custodian:** [[Gravewarden Orrin Varith]]  
**Affiliation:** Church of Pharasma  
**Purpose:** Burial and memorial grounds within the Garden  

Tucked in the Garden’s northern quarter, the **Silent Grove** serves as both cemetery and sanctuary.  It is said that souls find their first peace here before moving beyond the Spire’s shadow.  Even the most devout priests tread softly among its whispering trees.

---

## Description
White-barked trees rise among moss-covered gravestones.  Wind-bells hang from every branch, each tuned to a mourner’s name.  When the wind stills, the grove falls into a complete, unsettling silence — a silence that lingers even in thought.

A circular reflecting pool lies at the grove’s heart; flowers float on its surface, one for each recently departed.

---

## Access & Restrictions
Open during daylight.  The [[Sanctified Guard]] accompanies all processions and stands watch during rites.  
Necromancy and spirit communication are strictly forbidden; even [[Gravewarden Orrin Varith]] refuses resurrection requests on this consecrated soil.

---

## Encounters & Secrets
- **Gravewarden Orrin Varith:** The keeper-priest, who secretly believes the city’s dead do not truly leave.  
- **The Silent Bell:** When struck, makes no sound — yet drives away undead for a full day.  
- **The Rooted Tomb:** Beneath a massive birch lies an ancient sarcophagus entwined with living roots.

---

## Adventure Hooks
- **The Unending Wake:** Ghostly mourners appear nightly, reliving a funeral no one remembers.  
- **Petal and Bone:** Blossoms from the [[Fountain of Petals]] begin appearing on graves unbidden.  
- **Echo in the Pool:** A reflection shows a different sky — one without the Spire’s shadow.  

---

## Notes
To the faithful of Pharasma, the Silent Grove is where all questions end.  
To the rest of Respite, it is where the city remembers that even gods must one day fall silent.
