# 🏰 Respite, the Holy City-State
**Motto:** *“All faiths rise beneath the [[The Spire]].”*  
**Population:** ~500,000 within the walls, ~1.5 million in surrounding lands and satellite monasteries.  
**Government:** Theocratic city-state ruled by the [[Conclave of Faiths]] under the authority of the [[Pontifex of Respite]].  
**Current Pontifex:** [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]]  

---

## 📍 Location
Respite stands on the northern shore of the **Aurelion River**, overlooking the Inner Sea’s trade routes between distant kingdoms.  
The city’s founding site was chosen not for geography, but for divinity — the place where the [[The Spire]] erupted from the world four centuries ago.  
The river bisects the metropolis, flowing beneath bridges inscribed with sixteen divine seals and guarded by the [[Sanctified Guard]].

---

## 🛡️ Layout of Respite
A perfectly circular walled city built in divine geometry — concentric rings of faith, commerce, and industry surrounding the Spire.

### Inner Ring — The Crown
- **[[Crown of Faith]]** — Seat of divine governance, housing the [[Pontifex of Respite]], the [[Conclave of Faiths]], and the grand cathedrals of [[Shelyn]], [[Abadar]], and [[Iomedae]].  
- **[[Garden of the Gods]]** — A sacred park and neutral ground for all deities; public worship and funerary rites under [[Gozreh]] and [[Pharasma]]’s care.

### Middle Ring — The Working Circle
- **[[Hammergate]]** — Industrial heart and forge district under [[Torag]]’s blessing; every hammer strike a prayer.  
- **[[Halls of Relief]]** — Charitable and healing quarter governed by [[Sarenrae]], [[Erastil]], and [[Cayden Cailean]].  
- **[[River Aurelion Front]]** — Trade and dock district of [[Abadar]] and [[Gozreh]], connecting Respite’s wealth to the sea.  

### Outer Ring — The Living Faith
- **[[Pilgrims' Quarter]]** — Home to travelers, refugees, and reformers; birthplace of Serenya’s charities and her hidden Spiral cult.  
- **[[Scholarium]]** — District of knowledge and magic led by [[Nethys]] and [[Irori]]; center of law, invention, and divine academia.  
- **[[Crown Administrative Ward]]** — Bureaucratic core where divine decree becomes mortal law under [[Abadar]]’s oversight.  

### The Perimeter & Beneath
- **[[The Bastion Wall]]** — Immense fortress ring housing the [[Sanctified Guard]] and the Black Tribunal of [[Asmodeus]].  
- **[[Under-Spire]]** — Forbidden undercity of vaults, tunnels, and seals — the foundation of the divine lock imprisoning [[Rovagug]].  
- **[[Outer Respite]]** — Farmlands, shrines, and pilgrim villages sustained by [[Erastil]]’s clergy.

## 📖 Founding Myth & Hidden Truth
### The Faithful Legend
Four hundred years ago, during the Divine Schism, the gods united to heal the world.  
Where their light converged, [[The Spire]] rose from the earth, and the gods declared:  
> *“Here shall be peace. Here shall be Respite.”*  

The event became known as **The Covenant of the Gods**, commemorated yearly as the *Festival of Concord*.  
The faithful believe the Spire is the gods’ greatest miracle — a gift of renewal, proof of divine harmony.

### The Hidden Truth
The Spire was not a miracle, but a **seal**.  
Forged by the gods to imprison the Rough Beast [[Rovagug]], it anchors the planar lattice known as **The Annulus**, preventing his essence from consuming creation.  
Even the gods erased the memory of the act from their own minds.  

Centuries later, the city’s growth — its walls, railways, and holy circuits — unknowingly rewove the same geometry of containment.  
Now, the secret heresy of [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]] threatens to invert the pattern, turning lock into key.  

Her charities and architecture form a living sigil of unbinding — the **Rite of the Spiral**.

## ⚙️ Government & Faith
Respite’s divine hierarchy is both state and church:
- **[[Pontifex of Respite]]:** Supreme leader and mortal arbiter of divine will.  
- **[[Conclave of Faiths]]:** Sixteen high priests representing each major god of the Inner Sea.  
- **[[Sanctified Guard]]:** Unified military force under divine banners, ensuring peace through faith.  
- **[[Black Tribunal]]:** Asmodean inquisition enforcing obedience “for the good of unity.”  

No mortal crown exists here — only divine consensus, written in law and blood.
