# ⚓ The Guild Piers
The **Guild Piers** form a floating labyrinth of docks, bridges, and markets where trade never stops.  
Each pier belongs to a guild or faith, yet all answer to [[Abadar]]’s golden law.  
The air here is a chorus of bells, gulls, and the endless clatter of crates and coin.

---

## Features
- **The Merchant’s Hall:** Floating exchange where contracts are signed and disputes resolved.  
- **The Coin Chapel:** Small shrine where traders tithe a tenth of profit for “Abadar’s Blessing.”  
- **The Rust Market:** Black-market disguised as junkyard; technically legal under “salvage” law.  

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The Rust Market doubles as a meeting place for the Black Tribunal’s agents.  
- Contraband relics from the [[Under-Spire]] have begun surfacing here.  
- A merchant known as “Silverhands” trades divine materials to unknown buyers.
