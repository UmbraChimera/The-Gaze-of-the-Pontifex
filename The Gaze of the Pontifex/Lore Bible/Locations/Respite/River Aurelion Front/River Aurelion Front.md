# 🌊 River Aurelion Front
The **River Aurelion Front** is Respite’s mercantile artery — a vast, glittering expanse of docks, warehouses, and markets that stretch along the sacred River Aurelion.  
Here, ships glide beneath archways carved with divine runes of [[Gozreh]] and [[Abadar]], their sails painted with holy symbols and merchant crests alike.  
Faith, commerce, and weather itself bow to coin and tide.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~120,000 (dockworkers, merchants, sailors, smugglers, and pilgrims)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Abadar]], [[Gozreh]], [[Cayden Cailean]]  
- **Primary Function:** Trade, docks, shipping, mercantile regulation  
- **Atmosphere:** Bustling, humid, and alive with the scent of spice, salt, and ambition.  

---

## Architecture
Wooden warehouses rise alongside marble guildhalls. Steam-powered cranes creak beside divine windmills enchanted by Gozreh’s priests.  
Canals and aqueducts snake between tiers of docks, allowing trade ships to reach the city’s interior markets.  
At the heart of the waterfront stands the **Dockspire**, a massive lighthouse and shrine whose radiant beam mirrors the [[The Spire]]’s light into the river’s surface.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Archon Varrin Calistane]]** ([[Abadar]]) — Oversees trade contracts and harbor tariffs.  
- **[[Warden Tharo Stormleaf]]** ([[Gozreh]]) — Manages river sanctity, weather wards, and elemental tides.  
- **Guildmaster Renn Aurelion** — Mortal descendant of the city’s founder, representing the Merchant Compact.

---

## Economy
Trade never sleeps here.  
Every crate is blessed, every contract notarized, every ship tithed to Abadar’s temple.  
The **Guild Piers** function as miniature city-states of commerce — each ruled by faith and faction.  
The **Aurelion Exchange**, a marketplace of silks, spices, and relics, is one of the wealthiest locations in the Inner Sea.

---

## Faith Presence
- [[The Dockspire]] — Lighthouse-temple to Gozreh and Cayden Cailean, beacon for travelers and storm-worn sailors.  
- [[Aurelion Gate]] — Monumental entryway between Respite and the riverlands, guarded by Abadar’s Gilded Watch.  
- [[The Guild Piers]] — Dozens of floating chapels, shrines, and merchant barges sanctified for divine commerce.  

---

## Crime & Corruption
Smuggling thrives beneath sanctified trade.  
Pirates turned pilgrims find asylum under Cayden’s banner, and forged contracts are an art form in themselves.  
The **Black Tribunal** maintains informants here — their iron badges hidden beneath merchant robes.

> *“Gold buys the gods’ silence faster than prayer.”*

---
