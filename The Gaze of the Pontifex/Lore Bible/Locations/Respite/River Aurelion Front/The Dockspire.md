# 🕯️ The Dockspire
The **Dockspire** is both lighthouse and temple — a divine beacon dedicated to [[Gozreh]] and [[Cayden Cailean]].  
Its light mirrors [[The Spire]]’s energy into the waters, guiding ships safely to harbor.  
Sailors claim the beam hums in harmony with the city’s divine resonance.

---

## Features
- **The Beacon Flame:** Lit with alchemical oil blessed by both gods; turns blue when storms near.  
- **The Tide Hall:** Grand chamber where tide-priests predict the river’s moods using arcane instruments.  
- **The Captain’s Shrine:** A bar where toasts are made in Cayden’s honor before every voyage.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The Dockspire’s light once pulsed black for a night — it was seen as far as the Crown of Faith.  
- Hidden beneath its foundation is a chamber called **The Deep Mirror**, a portal into the reflection of the Aurelion below.  
- [[Warden Tharo Stormleaf]] forbids its mention; he claims the Mirror “dreams of drowning the sun.”
