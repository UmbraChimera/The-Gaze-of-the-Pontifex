# 🪙 Aurelion Gate
The **Aurelion Gate** is Respite’s only entryway — a colossal triumphal arch spanning the River Aurelion.  
Carved runes of [[Abadar]] bless every traveler and trade cart that passes beneath.  
The gate’s design mirrors [[The Spire]]: perfect symmetry, divine proportion, and eternal vigilance.

---

## Features
- **The Gatehouse Vault:** Abadar’s clerics weigh all incoming goods; corruption here is punished by divine flame.  
- **The Coin Arch:** Golden doors etched with trade laws of the Conclave, re-etched each decade.  
- **The Pilgrim’s Causeway:** A long bridge connecting Outer Respite to the inner walls, lined with statues of every god.  

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- Hidden beneath the Pilgrim’s Causeway is a tunnel used by smugglers and spies.  
- The runes of Abadar faintly pulse when spoken prayers of **Rovagug** are uttered nearby.  
- The arch’s keystone holds a relic called *The First Contract*, believed to have been signed by the gods after binding the Rough Beast.
