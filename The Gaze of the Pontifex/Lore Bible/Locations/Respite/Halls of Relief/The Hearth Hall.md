# 🏡 The Hearth Hall
*Erastilian community center for food, family, and education.*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Halls of Relief]]  
**Custodian:** High Elder Branik Feldt  
**Affiliation:** Church of Erastil, recognized by the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Public kitchen, schoolhouse, and communal hall devoted to the virtues of family, labor, and compassion  

Among the soaring temples of the Halls of Relief, the **Hearth Hall** appears humble — a wide brick longhouse surrounded by gardens and children’s laughter.  It is the oldest continually occupied building in the district and serves as **Respite’s beating heart of community**.  Here, the faithful of Erastil feed the hungry, teach trades, and host open councils for families displaced or in need.

---

## Description
Warm light spills constantly from wide arched windows.  The scent of bread and soup hangs in the air even at midnight.  
The structure is divided into three wings:

- **The Oven Hall:** Vast communal kitchen run by lay priests and volunteers.  The ovens are said to have never cooled in three centuries.  
- **The Table Court:** Banquet-style meeting hall where neighbors share meals and mediate disputes through food and fellowship.  
- **The Learning Loft:** A timber-built school above the kitchens, teaching letters, numbers, and crafts to any child regardless of birth or faith.

A great mural over the hearth depicts Erastil as a kindly farmer, hand extended toward a radiant city — the divine reaching out to the mortal hearth.

---

## Access & Restrictions
The Hall is open to all who labor or learn.  Visitors are expected to offer either coin, time, or teaching in return for the hospitality received.  
Weapons must be left at the entrance rack; disputes are settled only at the Table Court.  
During major holy days, the [[Sanctified Guard]] quietly assist in distributing food to the outer rings.

The Hall closes only for one hour before dawn, when its fires are ritually stoked anew.
