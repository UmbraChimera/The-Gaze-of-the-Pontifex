# 🌾 Halls of Relief
The **Halls of Relief** are the city’s heart of mercy — a radiant sprawl of hospitals, soup kitchens, and sanctuaries where every soul is welcomed.  
Here, faith is not ceremony but action; the weary are fed, the wounded healed, and the lost comforted.  
Light spills from every window, and the scent of bread and incense hangs eternal in the air.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~80,000 (the poor, pilgrims, healers, and charitable clergy)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Sarenrae]], [[Erastil]], [[Cayden Cailean]]  
- **Primary Function:** Healing, shelter, and charity  
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, bustling, and filled with hymns and gratitude  

---

## Architecture
The district’s architecture is simple but beautiful — curved domes, sun motifs, and open-air courtyards.  
Golden light from enchanted mirrors keeps even the darkest alleys illuminated.  
Streams of holy water from the [[The Spire]]’s runoff feed fountains of healing and reflection.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]** ([[Sarenrae]]) — Figurehead of charity, reform, and the district’s spiritual guide.  
- **[[Hearthwarden Hesta Fieldsong]]** ([[Erastil]]) — Oversees distribution of food and goods.  
- **[[Alewarden Ysella Bramblebrook]]** ([[Cayden Cailean]]) — Maintains morale, taverns, and communal festivals.  

---

## Economy
Charity sustains itself here. Donations flow through the temples, supported by Reformist funding from the Crown.  
Craftspeople donate time and goods, while merchants earn favor through visible generosity.  
The **Golden Sun Market** acts as both bazaar and redistribution hub.

---

## Faith Presence
- [[Cathedral of the Dawn]] — Grand temple of [[Sarenrae]], beacon of reform and mercy.  
- [[Hero's Rest]] — Tavern-temple of [[Cayden Cailean]], haven for pilgrims and soldiers.  
- [[The Hearth Hall]] — Erastilian community center for food, family, and education.
