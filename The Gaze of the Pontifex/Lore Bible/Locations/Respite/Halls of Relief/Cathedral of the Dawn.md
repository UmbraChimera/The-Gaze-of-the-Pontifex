# ☀️ Cathedral of the Dawn
*Grand temple of Sarenrae — beacon of reform and mercy.*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Halls of Relief]]  
**Custodian:** [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]  
**Affiliation:** Church of Sarenrae, seat upon the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Center of healing, forgiveness, and charitable reform  

Rising at the eastern edge of the Halls of Relief, the **Cathedral of the Dawn** greets each morning’s first light through an immense stained-glass oculus.  It stands as both sanctuary and hospital, its doors never closed, its flames never dimmed.  Within Respite’s hierarchy, it is celebrated as the purest expression of mercy — a place where even the condemned may seek absolution.

---

## Description
The cathedral’s spires are sheathed in gold leaf that catches sunrise and scatters it across the district like molten fire.  
Inside, the great nave is open to the sky, covered only by sheets of enchanted glass that warm but never burn.  
Every wall bears murals of rebirth — phoenixes, dawn blossoms, and broken chains transformed into halos.

- **Hall of Kindled Hearts:** Public sanctuary for confession and healing prayer.  
- **The Ember Aisle:** Inner corridor lined with braziers tended by clerics; each flame dedicated to a soul in need of forgiveness.  
- **Garden of Cinders:** A small courtyard where the ashes of the redeemed are sown beneath radiant flowers.  

A single continuous hymn, sung by unseen choirs, fills the air from sunrise to sunset.

---

## Access & Restrictions
Open to all; no titles or ranks are recognized within its nave.  
The [[Sanctified Guard]] maintains a ceremonial watch but does not interfere in confessions or penance.  
By decree of the [[Pontifex of Respite]], none may carry drawn steel within fifty paces of the central brazier.  
During the nightly *Rite of Dimming*, all fires are lowered save the central flame, which burns through the darkness.
