# 🍻 Hero’s Rest
*Temple of Cayden Cailean — God of Freedom, Bravery, and Ale*

---

## Identity
**District:** [[Halls of Relief]]  
**Custodian:** [[Alewarden Ysella Bramblebrook]]  
**Affiliation:** Church of Cayden Cailean, represented on the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Purpose:** Haven of courage, laughter, and restorative fellowship  

In the heart of the [[Halls of Relief]], amid hospitals and hospices, stands **Hero’s Rest** — a tavern-temple where joy is medicine and laughter divine.  Its priests minister not through sermons but shared cups, stories, and reckless hope.  The poor and wounded drink freely; the wealthy tithe heavily to keep the ale flowing.

---

## Description
The hall is built from reclaimed ship timbers, its walls carved with heroic scenes half-truth, half-legend.  A skylight of colored glass depicts Cayden’s ascent to godhood, casting amber light over the central bar-altar.

- **The Bravehall:** A vast common chamber where the sick and weary gather nightly to toast survival.  
- **The Cup of Trial:** A raised platform where visitors may drink the *Cayden’s Proof* — an enchanted brew that compels honesty and courage.  
- **The Quiet Cask:** A sanctuary for trauma-stricken soldiers, guarded by charm wards that suppress nightmares.  
- **The Fallen Step:** A stair whose risers bear the names of those who died in service or rescue; the last cup of every night is poured upon its stone.

---

## Access & Restrictions
All are welcome, and none are judged.  The only rule: **no coercion, no chains** — physical or spiritual.  
Healers from neighboring temples often send patients here when divine magic cannot mend what fear has broken.  
The [[Sanctified Guard]] unofficially patrol the door to prevent excess brawling; the [[Black Tribunal]] calls the place “a den of moral infection.”
