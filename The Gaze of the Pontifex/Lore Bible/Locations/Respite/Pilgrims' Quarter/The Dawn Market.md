# 🌅 The Dawn Market
Chaotic sprawl of stalls where sermons and sales pitches compete. Miracles auctioned as curiosities.

## Features
- **Fountain of Grace:** Blessed by [[Sarenrae]]; water glows faintly at noon.  
- **Sermon Steps:** Platform for prophets and charlatans.  
- **Traders’ Veil:** Canvas tunnels sheltering smugglers and informants.

## Secrets (GM Only)
- Murders of Desnan/Cayden priests; bodies found drowned in holy water.  
- Serenya’s “free stalls” form a hidden sigil in the market layout.  
- An old aqueduct beneath the fountain runs toward the [[Under-Spire]].
