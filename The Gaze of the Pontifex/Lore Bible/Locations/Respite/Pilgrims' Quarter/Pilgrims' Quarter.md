# 🏘️ Pilgrims' Quarter
The **Pilgrims’ Quarter** is Respite’s beating heart — a sprawling, crowded district where travelers, refugees, and laborers gather beneath the shadow of the [[The Spire]]. Faith here is raw and unpolished: sermons shouted from rooftops, prayers traded for bread, and miracles witnessed by the desperate.

It is both the city’s most alive and its most unstable district — the place where the gods’ promises meet mortal hunger.

## Overview
- **Population:** ~160,000 (laborers, pilgrims, merchants, transient faithful)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Cayden Cailean]], [[Desna]], [[Sarenrae]]  
- **Primary Function:** Housing, pilgrimage, travel, street markets  
- **Atmosphere:** Chaotic, vibrant, and perpetually hopeful

## Architecture
Wooden balconies lean over narrow cobbled streets, festooned with prayer ribbons. Public altars dot every corner. Above the rooftops rises the **[[The Charity Spire]]**, its golden dome reflecting the light of the true [[The Spire]].

## Leadership
- **[[Alewarden Ysella Bramblebrook]]** (Cayden Cailean) — hospitality, mediation among inns/taverns.  
- **[[Starseer Mirathiel Silver-Eye]]** (Desna) — guides pilgrims through dreams and portents.  
- **[[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]** (Sarenrae) — charities and orphanages scattered throughout.

## Economy
Inns, taverns, shrines, and carts. Faith is currency — a blessing can buy a bed or meal. The **[[The Dawn Market]]** doubles as bazaar and sermon ground.

## Faith Presence
- [[The Wayfarer's Inn]] — legendary inn where no traveler is refused.  
- [[The Charity Spire]] — Sarenrite hospice and food bank.  
- [[The Dawn Market]] — open plaza for trade, sermons, and public miracles.

## Crime & Corruption
Petty theft and exploitation abound. Fake priests sell “miracles.” Smugglers and heretics hide in the crowds. Desna’s **Silver Shields** patrol by starlight.

> *“Hope feeds the hungry, but hunger eats hope.”*

## Rumors
- A blind beggar preaches with eyes glowing like the Spire.  
- A caravan vanished inside the Charity Spire, leaving only echoes.  
- Many pilgrims share the same dream: a golden spiral pulling them underground.

## GM Hooks
- A pilgrim met Serenya here the same hour she spoke in the Crown.  
- A Desnan seer warns a “river of light” will burn/bless all it touches.  
- A tavern vanishes overnight; a perfect spiral scorches the flagstones.
