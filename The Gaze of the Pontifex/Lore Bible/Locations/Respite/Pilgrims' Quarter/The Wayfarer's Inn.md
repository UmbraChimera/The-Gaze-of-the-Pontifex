# 🛏️ The Wayfarer's Inn
A sprawling tavern-temple of [[Cayden Cailean]] with the creed: **“No traveler turned away, no story left untold.”** Rooms seem to rearrange at night, ensuring there’s always space.

## Features
- **Everfire Hearth:** Never-dying fire with gentle divine warmth.  
- **Hall of Tales:** Carvings of notable pilgrims through the ages.  
- **Cellar of Spirits:** Blessed casks said to ward nightmares.
