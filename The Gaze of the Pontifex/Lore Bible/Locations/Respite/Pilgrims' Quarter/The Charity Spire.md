# 🌞 The Charity Spire
Tallest structure in the Quarter — a radiant hospice and cathedral founded by [[Dawnmother Serenya Vaelith]]. Houses the poor, sick, and orphaned.

## Features
- **Hall of Mercy:** Vast clinic lined with stained glass miracles.  
- **Orphan’s Ascent:** Spiral stair said to cleanse the heart when climbed barefoot.  
- **Solarium:** Upper chamber radiant even at midnight.

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The Solarium is part of the **Rite of the Spiral**.  
- One **Spiral Vault** lies beneath the foundations.  
- Orphans sometimes speak in unison, reciting unknown “prayers.”
