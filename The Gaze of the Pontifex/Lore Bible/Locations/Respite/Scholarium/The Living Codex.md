# 📜 The Living Codex
A self-writing scripture that updates Respite’s laws in real time. New entries appear in ink of shifting color — black for mortal decrees, gold for divine rulings.

## Features
- **The Turning Page:** A leaf that flips itself when new law is made.  
- **The Margin of Appeals:** Notes of pending cases shimmer until resolved.  
- **The Glossary of Saints:** Names that brighten when their virtues are invoked.

## Secrets (GM Only)
- Names vanish when certain prayers to the *Chained God* are whispered.  
- A golden entry recently appeared with no author — a law that binds no one yet.  
- The Codex knows who will be Pontifex next; the page is sealed in wax.
