# 🖋️ Monastery of the Perfect Quill
Irori’s serene archive-monastery where truth is copied until it is perfected. Silence here is a discipline; errors are confessed as sins.

## Features
- **The Scriptorium:** Rows of monks writing in unison.  
- **The Breath Hall:** Meditation chamber aligned to the Spire’s hum.  
- **The Vault of Errata:** A sealed archive of mistakes never to be repeated.
