# ✨ Hall of Refraction
Nethys’s tower of magical law. A prismatic court where spells are weighed like arguments and precedent glows in glass.

## Features
- **The Prism Court:** Judgments rendered as light patterns inscribed into crystal panes.  
- **The Null Chamber:** Anti-magic vault for evidence.  
- **The Refracted Archive:** Case law recorded in living glass.
