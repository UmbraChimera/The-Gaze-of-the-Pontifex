# 🪙 The Registrar’s Hall
The **Registrar’s Hall** handles births, deaths, contracts, and vows across Respite.  
It is said every citizen’s name — living or dead — is written here at least once.

---

## Features
- **Hall of Names:** Endless archives of recorded souls, magically sorted by faith.  
- **The Oath Chamber:** Marble dais where oaths are bound by light.  
- **Memory Vault:** Repository for contracts with divine implications.
