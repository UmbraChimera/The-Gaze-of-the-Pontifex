# 🏛️ The Bureau of Divine Decrees
The **Bureau of Divine Decrees** is the core archive of Respite’s legal system — a sprawling cathedral of scrolls where mortal bureaucracy meets divine revelation.  
Its corridors stretch for miles, lined with shelves of living parchment that rewrite themselves as laws change.

---

## Features
- **The Vault of Judgments:** Sealed chamber containing every divine law enacted since the city’s founding.  
- **The Whispering Ink:** Animated ink used for dictation; it occasionally writes on its own.  
- **The Witness Bell:** Rings whenever a law is broken within the city walls.
