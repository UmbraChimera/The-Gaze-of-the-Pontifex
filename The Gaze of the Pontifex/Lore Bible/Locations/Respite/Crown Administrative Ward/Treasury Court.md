# 💰 The Treasury Court
The **Treasury Court** functions as both temple and tribunal of [[Abadar]].  
It is where economic sins — theft, fraud, tax evasion — are tried as divine crimes.  
Every verdict is etched in gold leaf across the courtroom’s walls.

---

## Features
- **The Chamber of Weights:** Balances that judge both gold and guilt.  
- **The Gilded Bench:** Judges sit upon coin-thrones that rise or fall with their favor in Abadar’s sight.  
- **Vault of Fines:** Treasury that fills and empties itself according to divine will.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- A hidden sub-ledger lists payments not made in coin, but in souls.  
- An infernal contract discovered here was written in the Pontifex’s hand — and signed in his blood.  
- A door behind the Chamber of Weights opens only when two judges disagree.
