# 🕳️ Under-Spire
Beneath [[Respite]] lies the **Under-Spire** — a vast labyrinth of ancient vaults, collapsed tunnels, and forbidden sanctums built around the foundations of [[The Spire]].  
Officially, it is a restricted zone. In truth, it is a wound — the closest mortals can come to the Chained God’s prison.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~1,000 authorized workers; many more illegally.  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Pharasma]], [[Nethys]]  
- **Hidden Faith:** [[Rovagug]]  
- **Primary Function:** Containment, maintenance, and burial of divine artifacts.  
- **Atmosphere:** Silent, cold, echoing with distant machinery and whispers.

---

## Architecture
The Under-Spire’s tunnels are ancient — older than Respite itself.  
Black metal veins run through the stone like frozen lightning.  
Some walls pulse faintly in rhythm with the Spire above.  
Pharasmin lanterns illuminate miles of sealed doors marked only by sigils of warning.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Gravewarden Orrin Varith]]** ([[Pharasma]]) — Oversees containment and funerary rites.  
- **[[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis]]** ([[Nethys]]) — Regulates magical access and study.  
- **Custodian Varr Telren** — Mortal warden, scarred from exposure to “resonant light.”

---

## Faith Presence
- [[The Annulus]] — Central seal chamber; divine lattice containing [[Rovagug]].  
- [[The Spiral Vaults]] — Hidden network used by the cult of [[Serenya Vaelith]].  
- [[The Garden of Chains]] — Pharasmin meditation hall built above sealed tunnels.

---

## Economy
None official. Artifacts recovered here are priceless — or cursed.  
Illicit trade in relics and forbidden texts funds both scholars and heretics.

---

## Crime & Corruption
The most sacred law of Respite forbids unlicensed descent — yet expeditions occur nightly.  
The [[Black Tribunal]]’s guards patrol to keep knowledge buried, but even they fear to go too deep.

> *“The Spire is the lock. The Under-Spire is the keyhole.”*

---

## GM Hooks
