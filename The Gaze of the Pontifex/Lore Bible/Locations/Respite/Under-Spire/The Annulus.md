# 🔶 The Annulus
*Central seal chamber; divine lattice sustaining the city’s harmony.*

---

## Identity
**Region:** [[Under-Spire]]  
**Custodian:** [[Sanctified Guard]] under mandate of the [[Conclave of Faiths]]  
**Affiliation:** Pontifical Domain; no single faith claims ownership  
**Purpose:** Divine harmonic nexus maintaining the balance of Respite  

Hidden deep beneath the Spire’s foundations lies **The Annulus**, an ancient chamber said to hum with celestial resonance.  
It is spoken of only in theological circles and official edicts as *“the heart that steadies the city.”*  Scholars describe it as both artifact and space — a perfect ring of radiant stone, etched with sixteen divine runes representing unity among the gods.

---

## Description
The Annulus forms a circular vault of unknown depth, lined with luminous script that shifts between divine languages.  
The air vibrates faintly, a tone too deep for mortal ears.  Within its circumference stand sixteen statues — each faceless, carved from a different celestial metal.  Light flows between them in a slow spiral, pulsing once every heartbeat.

Clerics permitted entry describe sensations of weightlessness, as if the laws of matter are thinner there.  Every sound falls into harmony; even whispers resolve into chords.

---

## Access & Restrictions
Access to the Annulus is **strictly prohibited** except by decree of the [[Pontifex of Respite]] and unanimous assent of the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
The [[Sanctified Guard]] maintain perpetual watch at its sealed gates beneath the [[Under-Spire]].  

Unauthorized entry is heresy of the highest order — “Trespass upon the Heart.”  
Few living have seen it; fewer still speak of the experience.

---

## Encounters & Secrets
- **The Seal Wardens:** Chosen members of the Sanctified Guard bound by sacred vow; their tongues are ritually silenced upon appointment.  
- **The Harmonic Core:** An unseen source of power that sustains the divine resonance of the city — responsible for the faint, ever-present hum heard across the Spire.  
- **The Silent Sigil:** A seventeenth symbol carved into the floor, eroded almost beyond recognition, its meaning erased from every scripture.  
- **Residual Miracles:** Spells cast near the Annulus sometimes manifest with unintended brilliance or perfect symmetry.

---

## Adventure Hooks
- **The Harmonic Fracture:** A tremor in the Spire disrupts divine magic citywide; the PCs must escort engineers and priests to inspect the Annulus.  
- **The Vanished Warden:** One of the Seal Wardens disappears — leaving behind a song that cannot be transcribed.  
- **Echoes of Perfection:** An artificer claims to have replicated the Annulus’ harmonic field in miniature; the experiment draws dangerous attention from rival faiths.

---

## Notes
The Annulus stands as both relic and mystery — the axis upon which Respite turns.  
To most citizens it is myth, a metaphor for divine unity.  
To those who guard it, it is the unseen truth that must never fracture.
