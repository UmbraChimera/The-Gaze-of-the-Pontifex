# 🕯️ Shrine of the Gilded Flame
A modest yet immaculate chapel to [[Abadar]], the **Shrine of the Gilded Flame** burns at the heart of Hammergate’s commerce quarter.  
Clerics here bless new trade pacts, sanctify warehouses, and oversee ethical labor under divine contract.

---

## Features
- **The Golden Brazier:** Its fire consumes false oaths; liars cough gold dust for days after exposure.  
- **The Ledger Altar:** Publicly accessible records of every forge’s legal output, updated magically each dawn.  
- **Hall of Balances:** Where trade disputes are arbitrated by clerics wielding enchanted scales.
