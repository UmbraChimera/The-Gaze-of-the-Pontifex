# ⚙️ Hammergate
The **Hammergate** district is the roaring engine of [[Respite]].  
Here, molten faith pours into anvils of iron and invention. Every hammer strike is a prayer, and every spark that flies from the forges is a fragment of divine fire.  
The air never cools — it hums with labor, song, and the faint metallic echo of the [[The Spire]]’s heartbeat beneath the stone.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~100,000 (dwarves, humans, tieflings, and gnomes)  
- **Dominant Faith:** [[Torag]]  
- **Supporting Faiths:** [[Abadar]], [[Gorum]], [[Erastil]]  
- **Primary Function:** Industry, crafting, engineering, and innovation  
- **Atmosphere:** Loud, devout, and proud — a sanctified factory floor under the gaze of the gods.  

---

## Architecture
Great brick halls, iron arches, and stone chimneys dominate the skyline.  
Sacred furnaces glow blue-white with divine blessing.  
Massive chains hang between foundries, supporting suspended walkways where sermons are shouted over the thunder of the forges.  

The **Forge Roads**, paved with slag and soot-black marble, radiate outward toward the [[Crown of Faith]], mirroring the veins of a living machine.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Forgefather  Korrim Anvilsine]]** ([[Torag]]) — Oversees the forge-temples, worker unions, and the Iron Sentinels.  
- **Master Engraver Durok Emberbrand** — Leader of the Hammergate Unionists, a guild of craftsfolk fiercely loyal to Torag’s ideals.  
- **Chief Architect Theon Marcell** ([[Abadar]]) — Handles trade and taxation records; ensures each forge’s output serves the city’s infrastructure.  

---

## Economy
Hammergate’s production defines Respite’s wealth: sanctified arms, armor, machinery, and divine materials for the [[Sanctified Guard]].  
Its forges supply the [[Crown of Faith]]’s ornamentation and the [[River Aurelion Front]]’s merchant fleets.  
Every finished tool, every nail, carries a prayer burned into its metal: *“Duty is strength.”*

---

## Faith Presence
- [[The Anvil Works]] — Torag’s grand forge-temple and center of industrial worship.  
- [[Shrine of the Gilded Flame]] — Abadaran oversight chapel for trade ethics.  
- [[The Crimson Yard]] — Gorum’s dueling grounds and training site for soldiers.  
- **Union of Ember Hall** — Secular guildhouse secretly guided by Korrim’s followers; rumored meeting point for Reform sympathizers.  

---

## Crime & Corruption
Hammergate is the furnace of both virtue and vice.  
Black-market smelters divert materials into underground caches, and labor guilds sometimes clash violently with the priesthood.  
“Forge accidents” conveniently remove those who question quotas.  
The [[Sanctified Guard]]’s **Iron Sentinels** maintain order, but every fire hides whispers.

> *“In Hammergate, everyone’s faith burns — some brighter, some faster.”*

---

## Rumors
- *A hidden forge produces weapons that hum with divine resonance — tools of the gods themselves.*  
- *A half-molten statue beneath the Anvil Works weeps glowing metal that never cools.*  
- *An unmarked shipment of chain has vanished — some say it was meant for something bound beneath the [[The Spire]].*

## GM Hooks
- A prototype war machine forged under divine inspiration has gone missing.  
- Union unrest threatens to collapse the city’s weapons supply unless the PCs mediate or expose saboteurs.  
- A mysterious “blue fire” erupts in the foundries at night, drawing the PCs into a divine resonance mystery tied to the [[The Spire]].
