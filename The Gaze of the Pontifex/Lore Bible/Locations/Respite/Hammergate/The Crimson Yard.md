# ⚔️ The Crimson Yard
The **Crimson Yard** is the martial temple of [[Gorum]] and training ground of the [[Sanctified Guard]]’s **Crimson Host**.  
Here, worship takes the form of combat. The clang of blades against armor is as sacred as prayer.

---

## Features
- **The Field of Iron:** Central arena for sparring and divine duels; the sand permanently stained red.  
- **The Armory of Glory:** Repository for weapons consecrated in Gorum’s name; each blade hums faintly in its rack.  
- **The Blood Gate:** A massive iron arch through which initiates must fight their way to earn the title of “Warmade.”
