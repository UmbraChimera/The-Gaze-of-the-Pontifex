# 🔨 The Anvil Works
The **Anvil Works** is both temple and engine — the grand forge of [[Torag]], where devotion is measured in sparks and sweat.  
Here, the hammer’s rhythm is prayer, and molten iron flows like faith reborn.

---

## Features
- **The Great Forge:** A divine furnace said to have burned since the city’s founding, fueled by the heart of the Spire’s energy.  
- **The Chanting Hall:** Laborers sing hymns timed to the hammer’s beat; the sound echoes like thunder through the whole district.  
- **The Hall of Sparks:** A sanctum for inventions; every creation is blessed before it leaves the forge.
