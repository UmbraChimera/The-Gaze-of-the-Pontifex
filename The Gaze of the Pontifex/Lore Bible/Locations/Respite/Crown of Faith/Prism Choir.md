# 🎨 The Prism Choir
The **Prism Choir** is the grand cathedral of [[Shelyn]] and the residence of [[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]].  
Sunlight through thousands of stained glass panels paints the air with color. The sound of the choir never ceases; day and night, voices harmonize in perfect intervals that resonate with [[The Spire]] itself.

---

## Features
- **The Rosehall:** Central nave lined with living murals that shift with the seasons.  
- **The Hall of Echoes:** A vaulted chamber said to replay the prayers of the devout as faint melodies.  
- **Pontifical Garden:** Hidden courtyard of marble sculptures that bloom with divine light each dawn.
