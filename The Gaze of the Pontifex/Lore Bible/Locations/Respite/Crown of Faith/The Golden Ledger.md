# 🪙 The Golden Ledger
The **Golden Ledger** is both temple and treasury to [[Abadar]], deity of law, wealth, and civilization.  
Its walls are lined with ledgers etched into brass, each line a transaction blessed by the clerics of the Gilded Watch.

---

## Features
- **The Vault of Truth:** An enormous clockwork vault where all city contracts are stored and magically verified.  
- **The Exchange Hall:** Where currency, oaths, and promises are weighed and valued equally.  
- **The Court of Coin:** Arbitration chamber for trade disputes and divine audits.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- [[Archon Varrin Calistane]] maintains a secret vault beneath the temple: The “Silent Account,” containing debts owed by Conclave members.  
- Some ledgers occasionally rewrite themselves — believed to be Abadar’s correction of moral imbalance.  
- Every vault key has a twin kept deep in the [[Under-Spire]].
