# ⚔️ The Hall of Scales
The **Hall of Scales** serves as the supreme court of [[Respite]], presided over by [[High Justiciar Arria Varus]] and her Blades of Valor.  
Trials here are both judicial and spiritual; oaths are sworn under divine scrutiny, and lies burn the tongue like acid.

---

## Features
- **The Sword Dias:** A blade of light suspended over the accused. If they lie, it lowers by one inch.  
- **The Gallery of Virtues:** Stained glass portraits of saints of justice that glow when truth is spoken.  
- **The Judgment Hall:** Large enough to host duels of divine arbitration between clergy.
