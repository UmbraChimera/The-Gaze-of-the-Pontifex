# ⚖️ Hall of Concord
The **Hall of Concord** is the meeting place of the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
It serves as both parliament and courtroom, where the voices of sixteen gods echo in divine argument beneath the Spire’s shadow.

---

## Features
- **The Circle of Seats:** Sixteen golden thrones, each engraved with its deity’s sigil. The seventeenth remains empty — its symbol erased.  
- **The Celestial Dome:** A painted ceiling that subtly changes to mirror the current balance of divine power.  
- **The Hall of Reflection:** Mirrors enchanted to record debates in perfect memory.

---

## Secrets (GM Only)
- The erased seventeenth sigil occasionally reappears — a spiral of red light that vanishes when noticed.  
- [[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis]] suspects the mirrors are beginning to *speak back*.  
- A hidden passage connects directly to the [[The Spire]], sealed by divine law.
