# 🏛️ Crown of Faith
The **Crown of Faith** is the innermost ring of [[Respite]], the radiant core of divine governance.  
Here, artistry is law, devotion is politics, and perfection is both ideal and prison.  
Every street leads toward [[The Spire]], whose reflection glows in the polished marble streets.

The district embodies Shelyn’s beauty, Abadar’s order, and Iomedae’s justice — a trinity of faith that sustains the city’s theocracy.

---

## Overview
- **Population:** ~45,000 (clergy, nobles, and temple artisans)  
- **Dominant Faiths:** [[Shelyn]], [[Abadar]], [[Iomedae]]  
- **Primary Function:** Government, art, and divine administration  
- **Atmosphere:** Harmonious, ceremonial, and almost blindingly pristine  

---

## Architecture
A vision of divine symmetry.  
Every arch is mathematically perfect, every mural depicts unity and light.  
Streets form radial patterns converging on the [[Hall of Concord]], which glows with faint divine radiance when the Conclave votes.  
The air hums with invisible psalms — a byproduct of centuries of layered blessings.

---

## Leadership
- **[[Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]]** ([[Shelyn]]) — Pontifex of Respite and arbiter of divine unity.  
- **[[Archon Varrin Calistane]]** ([[Abadar]]) — Oversees trade, taxation, and temple law.  
- **[[High Justiciar Arria Varus]]** ([[Iomedae]]) — Commander of the Blades of Valor and judge of divine trial.  

These three are collectively called **The Triarchs of the Crown**, and no law passes without their joint approval.

---

## Economy
Wealth here flows from art, law, and patronage.  
Gold changes hands through commissions — statues, frescoes, and legal codices.  
[[The Golden Ledger]] manages all tithes and contracts, while artisans of the [[Prism Choir]] produce masterpieces “for the glory of unity.”

---

## Faith Presence
The Crown holds Respite’s most powerful temples:  
- [[The Prism Choir]] — Cathedral of Shelyn and seat of the Pontifex.  
- [[Hall of Concord]] — Meeting place of the [[Conclave of Faiths]].  
- [[The Golden Ledger]] — Treasury and legal vault of Abadar.  
- [[The Hall of Scales]] — Tribunal of Iomedae’s faithful and site of divine duels.

---

## Crime & Corruption
Open crime is rare — punishment is immediate and public.  
Corruption, however, thrives in refinement.  
Contracts written in divine script can hide mortal greed, and even a bribe here arrives in a velvet envelope sealed with gold wax.

> *“In the Crown, sins are sung, not shouted.”*
