# 📚 Oghma — The Binder of What Is Known
God of knowledge, invention, and inspiration.  
In **Respite**, Oghma’s clergy record every decree, invention, and law passed through the Conclave, ensuring no word of power is ever lost.

- **Alignment:** True Neutral  
- **Domains:** Knowledge  
- **Symbol:** Blank scroll  
- **Colors:** White, blue, and bronze  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Oghma_Daelen|Loremaster Daelen Quillborn]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Loreguard  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “What Oghma writes, the world remembers.”  
Citizens regard the Loreguard as impartial scribes; politicians see them as dangerous archivists.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Knowledge is sacred; ignorance is decay.  
- Record truth without judgment.  
- Inspire invention, but guard against hubris.  
- Every discovery must serve understanding.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Scribe*, *Archivist*, *Loremaster*.  
- **Structure:** Monastic archives led by a council of senior scribes.  
- **Duties:** Preserve Conclave minutes, catalog relics, and oversee education in the Loreward Colleges.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Blue-trimmed robes with blank scroll pins; ink-stained left sleeves.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Collaborates with [[NPCs/Conclave/Mystra_Pelbranth|Mystra]]; mistrusted by [[NPCs/Conclave/Bane_Korven|Bane]] for public transparency.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **The Ink’s Blessing:** Dedication before transcription.  
- **Festival of Illumination:** Annual release of public knowledge scrolls.  
- **The Quiet Quill:** A vow of silence during intense research.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hall of Words:** Oghma’s grand archive beside the Conclave Hall.  
- **Scribe’s Walk:** Street of parchment vendors and scholars.  
- **The Silent Repository:** Hidden vault of forbidden lore.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Oghma_Daelen|Daelen Quillborn]] — Bloc: Neutral.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Prioritizes records over rhetoric; votes for transparency.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Supports education, opposes secrecy in invention.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Oghma’s clergy preserve forgotten truths—including hints about the Spire’s true nature.  
- Their archives can reveal lost spells, political scandals, or suppressed prophecy.
