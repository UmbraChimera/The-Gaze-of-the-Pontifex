# 🌾 Chauntea — The Great Mother
Goddess of life, agriculture, and the bounty of the earth.  
In **Respite**, Chauntea’s clergy run soup kitchens, granaries, and farmlands beyond the walls. Her followers feed the city in both body and spirit.

- **Alignment:** Neutral Good  
- **Domains:** Life, Nature  
- **Symbol:** Sheaf of wheat or blooming rose on green field  
- **Colors:** Green, gold, brown  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Grainmother Alliera of the Fields]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Grainwardens  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “Where there’s bread, there’s Chauntea.”  
Beloved by commoners and farmers alike, her clergy are the most trusted hands in the Halls of Relief.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Nurture life wherever it grows.  
- Share bounty freely; hoarding breeds famine.  
- Honor the land and those who till it.  
- Violence is last resort; mercy sustains the harvest.

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Blessed Hand*, *Grainkeeper*, *Grainmother*.  
- **Structure:** Cooperative—decisions made by harvest councils.  
- **Duties:** Manage city food supply, bless bakeries, and oversee pilgrim farms.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Leather sleeves decorated with woven vine motifs; faint scent of grain and earth.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Close with [[NPCs/Conclave/Yondalla_Ysella|Yondalla’s]] halflings; wary of [[NPCs/Conclave/Moradin_Baelrik|Moradin’s]] industrial encroachment.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Blessing of the Fields:** Daily prayer of thanks over any meal.  
- **Harvest Home:** Late autumn feast shared across social classes.  
- **Day of Seeds:** Spring planting festival, paired with Chauntean marriage rites.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Breadmother’s Hall:** Main temple adjoining the Halls of Relief.  
- **The Green Hearth:** Communal kitchen serving pilgrims year-round.  
- **Farmer’s Market Shrine:** Public altar under a roof of vines.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Grainmother Alliera]] — Bloc: Reformer.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Progressive; supports food access and workers’ rights.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Opposes expansion that poisons soil or river.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Chauntea’s clergy to show kindness, grounding, and practical miracles.  
- Their faith forms a moral backbone among Reformers, especially against hunger or exploitation.
