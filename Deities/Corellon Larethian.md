# 🌟 Corellon Larethian — The First of Art and Magic
The patron of the elves and source of artistry, grace, and arcane mastery.  
In **Respite**, Corellon’s faith represents beauty made divine and the perfect order of creation; his followers dominate the city’s traditionalist bloc.

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
- **Domains:** Arcana, Light  
- **Symbol:** Silver crescent moon  
- **Colors:** Silver, blue, and white  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]]  
- **Primary Order:** The High Artisans and the Arcane Choir  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “All things of beauty owe Corellon their first breath.”  
His clergy are admired as cultured, noble, and insufferably proud.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Beauty and creation are acts of worship.  
- Magic is the highest expression of divine will.  
- Perfection is an endless pursuit.  
- Defend the elven legacy against corruption and decay.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Artisan*, *Chanter*, *Coronal*.  
- **Structure:** Rigid, hierarchical, based on artistic mastery.  
- **Duties:** Maintain the Spire’s artistic embellishments, preserve elven archives, and oversee magical wards.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Flowing silver-trimmed robes; sleeves and pauldrons etched with constellations.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Works with [[NPCs/Conclave/Mystra_Pelbranth|Mystra’s Magisters]] but often dismisses Reformers as “unrefined.”

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Rite of the First Stroke:** An artist’s blessing before a new work.  
- **Moonrise Vigil:** Night meditation under silver light.  
- **Symphony of Forms:** Communal art festival held twice a year, blending music and enchantment.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hall of Silver Echoes:** Great basilica-temple in the Garden of the Gods.  
- **Aurelion Gallery:** Museum-workshop attached to the Conclave Hall.  
- **Hidden Atelier:** Private studio beneath the Crown of Faith for spell research.

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Coronal Thalanil Aurelion]] — Bloc: Traditionalist.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Conservative, anti-industrial.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Opposes Hammergate’s growth; considers steam and smoke profane.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Corellon’s clergy emphasize formality and elegance.  
- Use them to highlight the tension between tradition and progress.  
- Their wards or art installations can conceal magical clues to the Spire’s true purpose.
