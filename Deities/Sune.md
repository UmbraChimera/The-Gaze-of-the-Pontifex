# 🌹 Sune — The Lady of Love
Goddess of beauty, passion, and love.  
In **Respite**, her clergy preside over art, fashion, and marriage—and wield influence far beyond the vanity of their image.

- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good  
- **Domains:** Life, Light  
- **Symbol:** Red-haired woman’s face or rose  
- **Colors:** Red, pink, and gold  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Sune_Selarra|High Radiance Selarra Flamehair]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Roseguard  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “Love is beauty; beauty is truth.”  
Her clergy are adored by the populace but derided by Traditionalists as frivolous.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Love freely and without shame.  
- Beauty is divine; cherish and create it.  
- Protect love from jealousy, hate, and cruelty.  
- Passion is not sin—it is life.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Radiant*, *High Radiance*, *Artisan of Heart*.  
- **Structure:** Loose; guided by emotional instinct.  
- **Duties:** Bless marriages, curate art festivals, and serve as cultural diplomats.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Bright silks, perfumed rose motifs; left sleeve adorned with painted flames or petals.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Allies with [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya]]; disdained by [[NPCs/Conclave/Tyr_Jorven|Tyr]].  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Rite of Blooming:** Exchange of vows or artistic dedications.  
- **Festival of the Rose:** Week-long celebration of art and affection.  
- **Flame’s Kiss:** Candlelit prayers for courage in love.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **The Crimson Court:** Cathedral of art and pleasure in the Garden of the Gods.  
- **The Gilded Balcony:** Theatre-temple overlooking Spirewalk.  
- **Hall of Petals:** Workshop and salon for artists.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Sune_Selarra|Selarra Flamehair]] — Bloc: Reformer.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Follows Reformers, swayed by charisma and appeal.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Opposes anything that uglifies the city; sponsors beautification projects.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Sune’s clergy to highlight culture and human connection amid politics.  
- Their influence can sway public morale and even Conclave votes through sheer charm.
