# ☠️ Kelemvor — Lord of the Dead
God of death, judgment, and the afterlife.  
In **Respite**, his priests tend crypts beneath the Spire and ensure proper burial for all faiths.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
- **Domains:** Death, Order  
- **Symbol:** Scales balanced on a skull  
- **Colors:** Black, gray, and silver  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[Gravewarden Caleth Veynar|Gravewarden Caleth Veynar]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Gravewardens  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “All debts are paid in silence.”  
The Gravewardens are respected but rarely loved—necessary custodians of mortality.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Death is natural and not to be feared.  
- The dead must be honored and laid to rest.  
- Undeath is a perversion to be destroyed.  
- Judgment must be impartial and final.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Mortifier*, *Gravewarden*, *High Judge*.  
- **Structure:** Orderly, with records for every burial.  
- **Duties:** Conduct funerals, maintain necropolises, and investigate restless dead.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Black robes trimmed in silver chains; left pauldron bears skeletal motif.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Works with [[Magister Pelbranth Mystralis|Mystra]]’s priests on arcane residue; mistrusts [[High Radiance Selarra Flamehair|Sune]]’s frivolity.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Last Rite:** Formal blessing at time of death.  
- **Festival of Lanterns:** Procession of lights to honor ancestors.  
- **Silent Vigil:** Priests stand one night each month without speech to honor stillness.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hall of Passing:** Grand mausoleum under the Crown of Faith.  
- **River Necropolis:** Catacombs along the Aurelion.  
- **Silent Steps:** Pilgrim walkway for mourning.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[Gravewarden Caleth Veynar|Gravewarden Caleth Veynar]] — Bloc: Wildcard.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Cautious; ensures balance between divine extremes.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Neutral but vigilant—fears the rail tunnels disturb old graves.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Kelemvor’s clergy can serve as both investigators and philosophers.  
- Their crypts are prime locations for eerie discoveries tied to Tharizdun’s lock beneath the city.
