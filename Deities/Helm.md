# 👁️ Helm — The Watcher
Lawful, tireless guardian of the innocent and the dutiful. Helm’s faithful are wardens, sentries, magistrates’ guards, and oath-bound soldiers who keep watch when others sleep. In **Respite**, Helm’s clergy run walls, gates, and night patrols; their presence is felt most strongly in the **Sanctified Guard** and at the river’s **Chain-Gate**.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
- **Cleric Domains (SRD):** **Life**, **Light**  
- **Symbol:** A staring eye set in a gauntlet  
- **Colors:** Steel blue, iron gray, white  
- **Seat on the [[Conclave of Faiths]]:** Represented by [[NPCs/Conclave/Helm_Selbrecht|High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil]]  
- **Primary Order in Respite:** [[The Sanctified Guard|Sanctified Guard]] — **Watchers Eternal** (≈10% of the Guard)

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “If the lamps are lit and the walls are manned, Helm is at his post.” Citizens respect Helmites as fair but unyielding; smugglers and agitators see them as the city’s unblinking eye.

---

## Tenets & Dogma (GM Brief)
- **Stand the Watch.** Do your duty without fail; vigilance is protection made manifest.  
- **Guard the Innocent.** Interpose yourself between harm and the helpless.  
- **Obey Just Authority.** Uphold rightful laws; refuse unlawful orders without disorder.  
- **Be the Wall.** Endure. Do not abandon your post for fear, bribe, or convenience.

*(Note: The SRD doesn’t provide Realms-specific deity dogma; the above tenets are campaign-original and rules-agnostic, paired with SRD domains.)*

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Watcher* (rank-and-file), *High Watcher* (city prelate), **High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil** sits the Conclave seat.  
- **Order:** **Watchers Eternal** — the Helmite arm within the [[The Sanctified Guard|Sanctified Guard]].  
- **Duties:** Walls and gates, curfew bells, border and harbor security (with Torm’s marshals on embassies).  
- **Uniform Cue:** Left arm/shoulder bears an **eye-in-gauntlet** motif over heavy plate; some wear crested helms tied into the shoulder design.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Works cleanly with [[NPCs/Conclave/Tyr_Jorven|Tyr’s Justiciars]] and [[NPCs/Conclave/Torm_Ydris|Torm’s Loyal Furies]]; clashes with reformist street preachers and smugglers policed at the [[Cities/Respite_Riverfront|Riverfront]].

---

## Worship & Rites (Local Practice)
- **The Third Watch:** Night vigil shared in shifts; novices recite oaths while pacing the walls.  
- **Lamp-Kindling:** Dusk rite—lanterns lit along the Spireward streets as Helmites call *“Eyes open.”*  
- **Oathbinding:** Public swearing of service; vambrace or gauntlet is touched to seal the vow.  
- **Fast of the Unblinking:** A brief, eyes-open prayer fast before major deployments or citywide alerts.

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Helm’s Gatehouse:** Fortress-shrine integrated into the western wall; armory, cells, and an elevated chapel with view over the **Spirewalk**.  
- **Chain-Gate Oratory:** Small riverfront sanctum overseeing the harbor barrier and night patrol musters.  
- **Watchers’ Lanterns:** Street-niche shrines maintained by residents; offerings are oil, clean rags, and lens glass.

---

## Relationships & Politics (Conclave)
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Helm_Selbrecht|High Watcher Selbrecht Ironveil]] — bloc: **Traditionalist**.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Pro-order, pro-curfew, anti-smuggling; backs Tyr on sedition cases, tempers Torm’s zeal with procedure.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Accepts Hammergate foundries if patrol integrity and worker safety are maintained; opposes unsupervised rail spurs beneath the **[[Cities/Spire|Spire]]**.

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Helmites to **frame consequences**: if something triggers a city lockdown, the Watchers Eternal run it by the book.  
- Their presence can **ratchet tension** without combat: lantern inspections, pass checks, quiet questions at gates.  
- When you need a neutral, law-driven voice, Selbrecht is the **calm, implacable** anchor.
