# 🏡 Yondalla — The Protector and Provider
Halfling goddess of hearth, family, and protection.  
In **Respite**, her clergy run pilgrim shelters and orphanages, acting as the moral heart of the Reformers.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Domains:** Life, Protection  
- **Symbol:** Cornucopia  
- **Colors:** Green, gold, and brown  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook|High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Hearthguard  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “Yondalla keeps the fire lit for all.”  
Her followers are adored for kindness and practicality; even the Pontifex cannot rule without their favor.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Guard the hearth and those who gather there.  
- Share blessings freely.  
- Never turn away the hungry or lost.  
- Family is sacred—blood or chosen.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Hearthkeeper*, *Matron*, *High Matron*.  
- **Structure:** Matriarchal councils running city shelters.  
- **Duties:** Oversee the Halls of Relief and pilgrim welfare.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Modest brown robes with golden trim and cornucopia brooches.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Strong bond with [[Grainmother Alliera|Chauntea]]; distrusts [[High Fist Korven Dreadmark|Bane]].  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Hearthlight Blessing:** Families share a lit candle and meal at dusk.  
- **Festival of Homecoming:** Annual reunion feast for lost or displaced pilgrims.  
- **Day of Warmth:** Winter celebration when no soul is left without shelter.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **The Warm Hearth:** Central orphanage and pilgrim hall.  
- **Hall of Tables:** Enormous communal dining hall in the Pilgrims’ Quarter.  
- **Yondalla’s Steps:** Shrine-lined street leading to the Halls of Relief.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook|High Matron Ysella Bramblebrook]] — Bloc: Reformer.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Votes for compassion and aid measures.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Neutral; values fair wages and worker safety.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Yondalla’s clergy to highlight faith as community service.  
- Her temples can act as havens, information hubs, or moral crossroads for PCs.
