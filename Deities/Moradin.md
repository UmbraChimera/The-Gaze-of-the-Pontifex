# ⛏️ Moradin — The Soul Forger
God of creation, smithing, and dwarves.  
In **Respite**, Moradin’s forge-priests dominate the Hammergate district, overseeing industry, rail construction, and civic engineering. Their craft sustains the city—and sparks the fires of political tension.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Domains:** Forge, Knowledge  
- **Symbol:** Hammer and anvil  
- **Colors:** Red, gray, gold  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Moradin_Baelrik|Forgefather Baelrik Anvilthane]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Forgeguard  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “When the forges burn, the city breathes.”  
Citizens respect the dwarves’ work ethic but fear the smoke and clamor that come with it.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Honor the forge; creation is sacred.  
- Temper strength with wisdom.  
- Protect the fruits of labor from corruption.  
- Build legacies that endure.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Forgehand*, *Hammerpriest*, *Forgefather*.  
- **Structure:** Guild-based, master-apprentice lineage.  
- **Duties:** Oversee smithing, rail forging, and industrial safety.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Blackened steel vambraces etched with hammer sigils.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Works with [[NPCs/Conclave/Torm_Ydris|Torm’s Loyal Furies]]; disputes with [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Chauntea]] over pollution.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Blessing of the Anvil:** Dedication before any major crafting.  
- **The Emberfast:** Annual festival marking the lighting of the city’s central forge.  
- **Rite of Cooling:** Commemorates completed projects with offerings of oil and iron.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Hammergate Forgehall:** Cathedral-forge at the heart of Hammergate.  
- **Moradin’s Ring:** Public plaza for dwarven ceremonies.  
- **The Ironfoundry:** Guild complex employing hundreds of artisans.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Moradin_Baelrik|Baelrik Anvilthane]] — Bloc: Traditionalist.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Conservative, prioritizes craftsmanship and stability.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Supports controlled progress; warns against untested machinery.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Moradin’s faith anchors the city’s industry.  
- His followers balance between progress and tradition—a vital tension for any Hammergate scenes.
