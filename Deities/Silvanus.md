# 🌳 Silvanus — The Forest Father
God of nature, balance, and renewal.  
In **Respite**, Silvanus’s druids guard the gardens and riverbanks, maintaining the fragile harmony between nature and faith’s stone sprawl.

- **Alignment:** True Neutral  
- **Domains:** Nature  
- **Symbol:** Oak leaf  
- **Colors:** Green, brown, gold  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Silvanus_Tharil|Warden Tharil Mossheart]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Verdant Wardens  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “The green still breathes beneath the stone.”  
Citizens respect Silvanites for serenity and practicality; Hammergate’s workers call them meddlers.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Nature must be preserved; balance above dominance.  
- Civilization and wilderness can coexist through respect.  
- Restore what is destroyed.  
- All life feeds the cycle.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Warden*, *Keeper*, *High Druid*.  
- **Structure:** Loose circle led by elder druids.  
- **Duties:** Maintain gardens, purify water, oversee river harvests.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Bark-hued robes; vine cords wrapped on the left arm.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Allies with [[NPCs/Conclave/Chauntea_Alliera|Chauntea]]; opposes [[NPCs/Conclave/Moradin_Baelrik|Moradin]]’s industrial excess.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Rite of Renewal:** Blessing of the seasons each equinox.  
- **Festival of Leaves:** Public planting of trees in Hammergate’s plazas.  
- **Circle of Stillness:** Monthly meditation in the Garden of the Gods.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **Grove of the Spire:** Sacred garden encircling the Conclave Hall.  
- **The Riverroot Sanctum:** Shrine carved into the Aurelion riverbank.  
- **Verdant Chapel:** Hidden courtyard between old aqueducts.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Silvanus_Tharil|Tharil Mossheart]] — Bloc: Neutral.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Supports moderation; rarely votes first.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Opposes deforestation and smoke; demands replanting oaths.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Silvanus’s druids are calm arbiters amid chaos.  
- Their groves and sanctums can be refuges—or battlegrounds—depending on how far industry spreads.
