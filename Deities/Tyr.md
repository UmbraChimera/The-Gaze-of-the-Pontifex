# ⚖️ Tyr — The Even-Handed
God of justice, fairness, and law. Tyr’s faith is the moral compass of **Respite**, ensuring divine law supersedes mortal politics.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Domains:** War, Order  
- **Symbol:** Balanced scales set on a warhammer  
- **Colors:** Silver and deep blue  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Tyr_Jorven|Justiciar Jorven Harth]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Justicars  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “Where Tyr walks, lies flee.”  
The Justicars are respected arbiters; their severity inspires fear and reverence in equal measure.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Uphold truth and fairness in all things.  
- Punish corruption and deceit.  
- Blind justice is the purest form of service.  
- Sacrifice comfort for righteousness.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Justicar*, *High Justicar*, *Judge*.  
- **Structure:** Strict hierarchy; all judgments are recorded and witnessed.  
- **Duties:** Oversee courts, trials, and investigations of treason.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Silvered armor with engraved scales; left arm bears a solid steel gauntlet.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Allied with [[NPCs/Conclave/Helm_Selbrecht|Helm’s Watchers]]; critical of [[NPCs/Conclave/Lathander_Serenya|Serenya’s]] populism.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Trial of Truth:** Confession ritual before Tyr’s effigy.  
- **Oathbinding:** Legal vows sealed with Tyr’s symbol.  
- **Festival of Scales:** Annual day of open court, where citizens may petition grievances.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **The Hall of Scales:** Central courthouse and temple in the Crown of Faith.  
- **Chamber of Balance:** Smaller tribunal used by Justicars for quick judgments.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Tyr_Jorven|Justiciar Jorven Harth]] — Bloc: Traditionalist.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Upholds law and precedent; opposes the Red Banner and anarchic reformers.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Approves only if regulated under temple contracts.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Use Tyr’s clergy as arbiters and moral test cases for PCs.  
- A Justicar’s ruling can advance or complicate political plots in Respite.
