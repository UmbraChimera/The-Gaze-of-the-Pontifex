# 🕷️ Bane — The Black Hand
God of tyranny, conquest, and controlled fear.  
In **Respite**, Bane’s presence is tolerated for balance—his soldiers, the Fists of Bane, act as grim enforcers under the Pontifex’s watch.

- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil  
- **Domains:** War, Order  
- **Symbol:** Black handprint  
- **Colors:** Black and green  
- **Seat on the [[Factions/Conclave of Faiths|Conclave of Faiths]]:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Bane_Korven|High Fist Korven Dreadmark]]  
- **Primary Order:** The Fists of Bane  

> **Public Sense (Respite):** “The Black Hand strikes so others need not.”  
Citizens fear Banites but admit crime dwindles where they patrol.

---

## Tenets & Dogma
- Control breeds peace.  
- Strength is proof of divine favor.  
- Fear preserves order.  
- Serve authority or crush rebellion.  

---

## Clergy & Organization in Respite
- **Titles:** *Fist*, *High Fist*, *Warlord*.  
- **Structure:** Militarized, authoritarian hierarchy.  
- **Duties:** Police subversive groups, enforce curfews, lead covert operations.  
- **Uniform Cue:** Black-green armor with spiked gauntlet on left arm.  
- **Allies & Friction:** Formally obey [[NPCs/Conclave/Corellon_Thalanil|Thalanil]]; loathed by Reformers.  

---

## Worship & Rites
- **Oath of Iron Rule:** Induction ceremony demanding absolute loyalty.  
- **The Silent March:** Procession through Pilgrims’ Quarter after executions.  
- **Night of Chains:** Private Banite holy day commemorating mastery through obedience.  

---

## Temples & Sites in Respite
- **The Iron Bastion:** Fortified chapel near the outer wall.  
- **The Chamber of the Black Hand:** Hidden interrogation hall beneath the Guard barracks.  
- **Silent Courtyard:** Memorial garden for Banite martyrs.  

---

## Relationships & Politics
- **Representative:** [[NPCs/Conclave/Bane_Korven|Korven Dreadmark]] — Bloc: Outsider.  
- **Voting Tendencies:** Votes for order, against reform.  
- **Stance on Industry:** Supports it if it strengthens control.  

---

## Notes for Play (GM)
- Bane’s faith introduces necessary menace and moral grayness into Respite’s politics.  
- His priests are a lawful evil counterbalance to the Reformers—perfect for intrigue or tension-building.
